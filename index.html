How to use
  • Drag empty space to look around the tank; wheel zooms; click empty space to deselect, double-click it for straight-on.
  • Drag a fish to move it: parallel to the glass when viewed from the front, across the floor plan from above, front/back from the side.
  • Split tank (Compare) makes an independent copy beside this one; tick Edit on a view to edit that tank (both ticked: both change). The padlock between the views links or frees their cameras. Save and Download keep both tanks together; Download on a view's tab saves that tank on its own.
  • On a phone or tablet: drag with one finger to look around, pinch with two to zoom, drag with two to move the picture.
  • Shift + wheel moves the selected fish forward/back. Del deletes, F flips, Ctrl+D duplicates, Ctrl+Z undoes.
  • Fish are drawn at adult size. A fish further back looks smaller exactly as it would through real glass at the viewing distance below.
  • Your current tank is kept in this browser automatically. Save adds it, with a picture, to the tanks Open… lists; Download keeps a file to back up or share (browser saves are lost if site data is cleared).

Hardscape
Move
Click a piece in the tank to select it; drag it to move it. It rests on the ground or on the piece under it, and carries whatever rests on it. Shift + wheel or R turns it; + and − resize it; the arrow keys move it left / right and forward / back, Page Up / Down up and down. Moved that way it stays put anywhere in the water, passing through other pieces if you like.
