# 04. Underglass: Aquarium Planner (iOS / iPadOS)
Researched 2026-11-25. Confidence: low on user feedback (none found: no ratings shown, no Reddit, forum, YouTube or Product Hunt mentions turned up in two searches). Product description is solid, from its own pages.

## Snapshot
- App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/underglass-aquarium-planner/id6832778540 . Developer listed as Sang Huynh. Site: https://underglass.sanghuynhxuan.com/ (no company or maker name on it; support email domain sanghuynhxuan.com, https://underglass.sanghuynhxuan.com/support.html).
- Price: free with coin in-app purchases: 60 coins $1.99, 160 $4.99, 340 $9.99, 900 $24.99 (App Store page). Start with 100 coins, +10 per day. No ads, no account, no subscription.
- Status: brand new and active. Version 1.0 on Nov 12 and 1.1 on Nov 17 (2026, per the page's copyright). 374 MB. Needs iOS/iPadOS 18+, also runs on M-series Macs and Vision Pro as an iPad app (App Store page).
- Audience: tiny. The store page says it has not received enough ratings to show an overview (App Store page). No social accounts listed (site).

## What it does
From https://apps.apple.com/us/app/underglass-aquarium-planner/id6832778540 and https://underglass.sanghuynhxuan.com/ :
- 3D tank on a phone: brush-sculpt substrate hills, place stone, driftwood, decor; stems, carpets, rosettes; fish school and keep to their own water level.
- Tanks from nano cube to 180 cm (about 71 in); 21 sample layouts or empty tank.
- Summary panel: volume, fish count, incompatibility warnings, overcrowding check. 140+ fish, plant, stone and decor items with notes.
- "Living" tank: plants grow over real days, even app closed, need occasional trimming; Home and Lock Screen widgets.
- Save a photo or 10-second video, or share a tank file. Offline, on-device, 10 languages.
- v1.1: glass-style UI, richer plant and stone textures, smoother fish movement, larger text (App Store page).
- Site disclaimer tells users to check real fish and plant needs before buying.

## Complaints and weak spots
No user complaints found. Observations from its own pages only:
- Coin-gated content: rare fish, color strains and tanks 120 cm and up cost coins (https://underglass.sanghuynhxuan.com/support.html). A planning tool that paywalls tank sizes is an obvious friction point.
- Coins and unlocks live in iCloud Keychain; "Restore Purchases" cannot recover them (same support page), a likely source of future refund and review complaints.
- Apple-only, iOS 18+, 374 MB. No Android, no web.
- Maker is anonymous on the site; no roadmap or changelog beyond store notes.
