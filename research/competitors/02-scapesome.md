- URL: https://scapesome.com. Maker: not named (About page gives only a contact email and "Made for first scapes"): https://scapesome.com/about
- Platform: desktop browser, nothing to install (no phone/tablet claim). Free account required, 1 tank free: https://scapesome.com
- Price: Free = 100 credits on sign-in, 1 tank, 2 saved versions. Credit packs $9.99 / $18.99 / $27.99 (400 / 800 / 1,200 credits, valid 12 months). Plus $19.99/mo (1,200 credits, 10 tanks). Pro $39.99/mo (3,000 credits, 20 tanks). Monthly credits do not roll over: https://scapesome.com/pricing
- Status: active, 2026 copyright, blog assets dated up to 2026-10. No explicit launch or last-update date: https://scapesome.com
- Audience: no reviews, ratings, testimonials, social links, Reddit or forum threads, or Product Hunt entry found. Appears very new and small.

## What it does
From https://scapesome.com unless noted:
- Planted-tank only (freshwater). Pick tank size, light, CO2, substrate, then hand-place hardscape, plants and fish in 3D.
- Library of 462 items: 188 plants, 160 fish/shrimp/snails, 32 rocks and driftwood (156 shapes), 48 substrates, 34 tank sizes.
- "Grown-in" AI images: preview the layout from month 0 to 12 from front, side, angle or top; each image tied to a layout version. Costs credits.
- Tank review (credits): temperature overlap, who eats whom, stocking level, plants unsuited to the light/CO2, plus follow-up questions.
- Planting suggestions: point at a spot and get library plants suited to your light and CO2, with reasons.
- Share card with 3D view or image, setup and item counts.
- Free calculators (volume, substrate, stocking) without an account; blog guides, e.g. https://scapesome.com/blog/aquarium-substrate-depth
- Directory page notes previews are for comparing ideas, not predicting biology: https://aisotools.com/tool/scapesome

## Complaints and weak spots
No user complaints found. Weak spots visible from its own pages only:
- Account required even for free planning; free tier is 1 tank and 2 saved versions (https://scapesome.com/pricing).
- The "grown-in" preview and tank review are pay-per-use AI credits; monthly credits expire (https://scapesome.com/pricing).
- Desktop browser only is stated; no mobile or AR mention (https://scapesome.com).
- Planted freshwater only; no reef, brackish, terrarium or herp content mentioned.
- No prices, shopping list, equipment or brand gear on the page; no maker identity shown.
- Growth is an AI-generated image, not a 3D simulation (https://aisotools.com/tool/scapesome).
