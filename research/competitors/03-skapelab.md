# 03. Skapelab (Web)
Researched 2026-11-25. Confidence: low (product is tiny and new; no third-party user feedback found anywhere).

## Snapshot
- URL: https://skapelab.com/ ("Aquascape Design Studio"). Maker: Skapelab LLC (page footer, 2026). A Florida LLC "SKAPELAB LLC", document L26000487987, is listed active on Sunbiz, but a search could not confirm it is the same entity (https://search.sunbiz.org/Inquiry/corporationsearch/SearchResults?inquiryType=EntityName&searchTerm=SKAPA+LLC).
- Platform: website only. Free, no account to create a design; saving designs and the PDF are also described as free; email sign-in links for an optional account. Revenue appears to be affiliate links (disclosed on the page). https://skapelab.com/
- Status: active as far as the page shows; no launch date, no changelog, no last-update date. The 2026 LLC filing suggests it launched this year.
- Audience size: unknown. No store listing, no ratings, no review counts. The site only has a per-design "Rate this design" option, not product reviews. https://skapelab.com/

## What it does
All from https://skapelab.com/:
- Seven-step design studio: tank size and technology level, visual style, stone and wood, livestock, colour palette, then a generated plan.
- Plan output: illustrated render, planting map with substrate depths, plant list, stocking compatibility check, care routine.
- Shopping list with approximate retailer prices and buy links; plan PDF.
- Standalone compatibility checker (fish, shrimp, snails) and a photo diagnosis tool for plant deficiencies, algae and water clarity (it says it does not assess fish health).
- Gallery; save, share and remix designs.
- Freshwater planted only. The sample build totals about $390 (tank, stand, equipment).

## Complaints and weak spots
No user complaints found. Observations from its own page only:
- The "render" is an illustration of a generated plan, not an orbitable 3D scene (page describes an "illustrated render"). https://skapelab.com/
- Equipment prices are described as approximate, not live. https://skapelab.com/
- Freshwater only; no marine, brackish, or terrarium worlds mentioned. https://skapelab.com/

## vs YourArium
- They have, we lack: photo diagnosis of plant/algae/water problems; a downloadable plan PDF with a planting map and substrate depths (we have a printable report); shareable gallery / remix; care routine (we deliberately skip care guides).
- We have, they lack: real-time orbitable 3D tank, animated fish from reference photos, marine and terrarium worlds, a calibrated stocking engine with "outgrows it in N months", owned-gear tracking, scheduled price/deal sweeps, A/B compare, budget vs luxury versions, experience-level gate.
- Ideas worth taking (ranked):
  1. Photo-based problem diagnosis (algae/deficiency) is a cheap-sounding hook, but it conflicts with the no-care-guides stance; only consider as an "identify" tool.
  2. Shareable design links plus gallery/remix as a growth loop.
  3. Planting map with substrate depth callouts in the printable report.
- Threat level: low. Brand-new, unproven, freshwater-only, no visible audience, and a 2D illustrated plan rather than a 3D simulator.
