# Competitor summary: YourArium vs 18 products (2026-11-25)

Per-product files: `01-*.md` to `18-*.md` in this folder; research brief and template: [_brief.md](_brief.md).
Cost: 18 Sonnet research agents at 80k-93k tokens each (about 1.53 M in all), plus about 116k for this synthesis.

Evidence warning: only the Steam games (06, 07, 15, 16) and the big iOS app (18) have real review volume (medium confidence). The rest are low confidence: most had no user reviews found anywhere, so "strength" and "weakness" below come largely from the products' own pages. Threat levels are the researchers' judgments, not measured. #04 Underglass could not be found under that name (site DNS fails, no store or forum hit); the file lists possible look-alikes (Vivarium: Nature Design, Shoal & Stem, TidyTank, CoralScape) but none are confirmed.

## 2. Recurring complaints (what customers still aren't happy with)

- **Uniform, unrealistic fish behavior.** Tetras that don't school, angelfish that aren't aggressive: [06](06-behind-glass.md), [07](07-aquarium-designer.md). Fish Game ([16](16-fish-game.md)) is praised precisely because it gets this right.
- **Missing shrimp, snails and other inverts.** The most repeated ask in [06](06-behind-glass.md), [07](07-aquarium-designer.md) and [16](16-fish-game.md).
- **Untrustworthy stocking numbers.** AqAdvisor percentages are "hocus-pocus" or non-linear and ignore plants ([17](17-aqadvisor.md)). My Aquarium Guide gallons are "off" and the list is thin ([18](18-my-aquarium-guide.md)). Aquabuildr's database accuracy is criticized ([08](08-aquabuildr.md)). Aquarium Designer has no overstock penalty ([07](07-aquarium-designer.md)).
- **Thin plant rosters and no layering or growth.** [10](10-scape-it.md) (missing biotope plants), [07](07-aquarium-designer.md) (fixed slots, no carpets), [06](06-behind-glass.md), [16](16-fish-game.md).
- **Clunky placement and missing editing basics.** Items land on the glass ([15](15-aquarist.md)); no undo, delete or duplicate ([07](07-aquarium-designer.md), [16](16-fish-game.md)); can't delete a tank ([18](18-my-aquarium-guide.md)).
- **Paywalls and monetization creep.** Packs and Pro ([18](18-my-aquarium-guide.md)), credits ([02](02-scapesome.md), [14](14-biotope-aquarium-planner.md)), subscription tank locks ([08](08-aquabuildr.md)), DLC ([06](06-behind-glass.md), [07](07-aquarium-designer.md), [15](15-aquarist.md), [16](16-fish-game.md)).
- **Games are not real planning.** Prices arbitrary, tank sizes unrealistic, little real-fishkeeping value ([07](07-aquarium-designer.md), [15](15-aquarist.md), [06](06-behind-glass.md)).
- **Bugs and performance** in the Steam titles ([06](06-behind-glass.md), [16](16-fish-game.md), [15](15-aquarist.md)); reminders that don't fire in [08](08-aquabuildr.md).

## 3. What users love

- Visuals and fish animation that look like a real tank ([06](06-behind-glass.md), [16](16-fish-game.md), [07](07-aquarium-designer.md)).
- Relaxing, ambient viewing; some use the tank as wall art ([06](06-behind-glass.md), [07](07-aquarium-designer.md), [15](15-aquarist.md)).
- Trying a setup before buying, and sandbox freedom ([06](06-behind-glass.md), [07](07-aquarium-designer.md)).
- Species-accurate behavior, with live "what is it doing" status ([16](16-fish-game.md)).
- Compatibility checks that stop bad pairings, beginner-friendly clarity ([18](18-my-aquarium-guide.md), [08](08-aquabuildr.md)).
- Fast, free, "general idea in minutes" planning with a wide hardscape choice ([10](10-scape-it.md)).
- Responsive developers and Discord ([16](16-fish-game.md), [08](08-aquabuildr.md)); no microtransactions in the base game ([16](16-fish-game.md)).
- A tool that inspires buying real livestock ([15](15-aquarist.md)).

Evidence note: praise for the web planners (01-03, 05, 09, 11-14) is essentially absent, not just thin.

## 5. YourArium's clear advantages (per the files, no other product matches all)

- Real-time orbitable 3D with real-brand gear in true colors: no competitor shows this; the closest 3D tools (01, 06, 11, 12, 16) have generic equipment or none.
- Calibrated stocking engine with gates and "outgrows it in N months": nobody else has growth-aware stocking; the closest (08, 13, 17) are single-number checkers.
- Marine, brackish, and terrarium/herp worlds in one tool; competitors with a visual tool are freshwater, with 01, 11 and 08 adding only reef or ponds.
- Real tank catalogue and sumps; Behind Glass users' top ask is custom sizes.
- Priced shopping list from real products with scheduled deal sweeps, Budget vs Luxury; closest are the approximate prices in 03 and the quantity list in 10.
- Free with no account, web plus phone; others gate by account (02, 05, 11), credits (02, 14), or subscription (08, 18).
- Guided setup, owned-gear tracking, A/B compare, printable report, experience gate.

## 6. YourArium's gaps, ranked by how valued

1. Live species-specific behavior and visible "what is it doing" status (06, 07, 16: top complaint or top praise).
2. Shareable links, gallery and remix (03, 10, 12, 13 all have or plan it; no sharing means no growth loop).
3. Plant growth over time (13 already ships a slider; 02 charges for it; planned for us).
4. Larger plant and species rosters (10, 13: 173 plants; 17: 917 species; 18: 1,000+); our counts are unchecked.
5. Native iOS app and store presence (08, 13, 18); we have web and Android.
6. Explainable stocking numbers and per-style design scores (17, 13).
7. Photo identification of fish (18) and photo diagnosis (03, 14): lower value, and partly out of scope.
8. Water chemistry, cycling, logging, reminders (08, 09, 13, 14): valued by retention-minded users but excluded by Nathan's decision.
9. Game loop, breeding, community challenges, photo mode, VR (15, 16, 06).
10. Substrate brush sculpting (12), if ours is weaker (unverified).

## 7. Top 10 ideas, ranked by value/effort

1. Explain every stocking percentage with a per-species breakdown, and publish where we differ from AqAdvisor (17; the top trust complaint; the engine already exists).
2. Show the live-plant effect on filtration/bioload visibly (17; AqAdvisor ignores it).
3. Species-specific swim, schooling and aggression behavior, kept distinct from the uniform-AI competitors (06, 07, 16).
4. Shareable read-only setup link, then a gallery (03, 05, 10, 11, 12, 13).
5. Surface juvenile vs adult size per species (18, from the engine's existing growth data).
6. Growth preview at month 0/3/6/12 once growth ships, free and 3D (02, 13).
7. Ambient "watch mode": hide UI, calm audio, second-screen use (07, 16).
8. Per-style design score (Iwagumi, Dutch, Jungle) with plain-language reasons (13).
9. "Click an empty spot, get plants for your light/CO2" with reasons (02).
10. Capacity meter with explicit warn/block thresholds, plus a "which filter fits" quiz as a gear entry point (08).

Runners-up: setup import/export (08), per-plant "pots needed" on the shopping list (10), public species pages for search traffic (05), photo fish ID (18), region biotope presets (14).

## 8. What to test hands-on first

1. **AqAdvisor (17):** our real benchmark and the trust complaint's source. Run the same stock lists through both and compare explanations.
2. **Fish Game (16):** the bar for fish behavior and live status. Medium-confidence files, shrinking but loyal base.
3. **App-aquatic planner (13):** free, no sign-up, and it already has growth, style scores and shareable links; the nearest same-audience planner (rated low-medium threat).
4. **Scape It (10):** share links, co-editing and gallery are its edge; it has the only real forum feedback on a web planner, though from Jan 2024, before v3.
5. **Behind Glass (06):** the realism bar for animation, and its review sample shows what a watching audience wants.

Reason to skip the rest first: 01, 03, 05, 09, 11, 12 are tiny with no user evidence; 02 is credit-gated (try its free tier for the grown-in preview only if testing growth); 08, 14 and 18 matter mainly as mobile retention and iPhone-search context.
