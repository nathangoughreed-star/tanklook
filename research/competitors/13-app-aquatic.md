# 13. App-aquatic (web planner + iOS/Android tracker app)
Researched 2026-11-25. Confidence: low (product found and described from its own pages and store listing; the only user feedback is 4 App Store reviews of the tracker app, none about the planner).

## Snapshot
- URL: https://app-aquatic.com/aquascape-planner.html. Maker: App-aquatic; App Store developer is Aaron David Minton (UK): https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6759092897
- Two parts: a free browser Aquascape Planner (marked "beta", no sign-up, last update not stated) and a free-with-IAP companion tracker app (iOS, Mac, Vision, Android per https://alternativeto.net/software/app-aquatic/about/).
- Price: planner free. App premium: store lists monthly/annual/lifetime ($79.99 lifetime) and a 14-day trial; two price sets shown, unclear which is current (App Store link above).
- Audience: iOS 4.9 stars from 18 ratings, v7.3.0, last update "Nov 4" (year not shown) (App Store link). AlternativeTo: 1 like, no comments. Small and new (copyright 2026).
- No Reddit, forum, YouTube or Product Hunt coverage found in two searches. A Shoal & Stem App Store listing reportedly mentions seeing an ad for it on Reddit, so it advertises there (search result, unverified).

## What it does
From https://app-aquatic.com/aquascape-planner.html:
- Tank setup: five common sizes, 140 tank models, or custom dimensions; light level and CO2 settings.
- Six styles (Nature, Iwagumi, Dutch, Jungle, Biotope, Low-tech), each with a starting layout and a style check scoring the design out of 100.
- 173 plants, 5 rock types, 5 wood types; drag-to-place with layering; substrate shaping (soil/sand/gravel, shapes and depths).
- Growth slider: planting day, 1, 3 and 6 months.
- Shopping list: plant pots and carpet cups, estimated rock weight, substrate volume, light/CO2/glue needs; save as image or copy a link.
- Plants too tall or wide for the tank are flagged; floor-space meter; designs saved in the browser; phone layout.
- Links to its stocking calculator (bioload, compatibility, water) pitched as an AqAdvisor alternative: https://alternativeto.net/software/app-aquatic/about/
Tracker app (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6759092897): tank drawn from a photo or manual setup, test-strip scanner, AI ID of fish/plants/algae, "What If" stocking preview, 800+ animals and 175 plants, cycle companion, reminders, CSV export.

## What users love
All from App Store reviews of the tracker (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6759092897), tiny sample:
- Keeps three tanks on schedule; compatibility checks and fast support praised.
- Water scanning caught a problem early; support team praised.
- "Simple and useful" for maintenance and reminders.
Nothing found about the planner.

## Complaints and weak spots
- One App Store review: logging water parameters was unintuitive, no multiple daily readings; the developer said that was coming (same link).
- A reviewer also notes a learning curve (same link).
- From its own page: planner is beta ("something may look wrong"); plants over tank size are hidden by default; saves tied to one browser unless a link is shared (https://app-aquatic.com/aquascape-planner.html).
- Scape planner is a layout aid, not a 3D animated scene; no livestock in the scape view is mentioned on the page.
- AI features are marketing copy, untested independently.

## What users wish it had
- Multiple daily water tests and better parameter history (App Store review above). Nothing found for the planner.
