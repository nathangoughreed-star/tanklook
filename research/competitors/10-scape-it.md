# 10. Scape It (web)
Researched 2026-11-25. Confidence: low-medium (product and its own claims well covered; user feedback is thin: one 2024 forum thread plus a roundup; Reddit, Product Hunt and YouTube comments not found).

## Snapshot
- URL: https://scape-it.io/ . Maker: Scape It UG (haftungsbeschränkt), Germany; developers named Luca Freiensehner and Henning Möllers (https://scape-it.io/).
- Platform: browser, desktop and mobile. Price: free to use; the home page links a "Scape It Pro" upgrade but gives no price (https://scape-it.io/). The /pro path returned a 404 when fetched.
- Status: active. The page says version 3.3 was released Oct 17, 2026 (https://scape-it.io/).
- Audience: small but real community. Has Discord, Instagram, Pinterest, YouTube (@scape-it), Facebook; lists aquarium YouTubers and Liquid Nature as supporters (https://scape-it.io/). No store ratings or review counts exist (web tool).
- Was launched as a free tool "days before" its creator's UKAPS post (https://ukaps.org/forum/threads/digital-aquascaping-what-do-you-think.62954/, via search summary; page itself blocked, 403).

## What it does
From https://scape-it.io/ unless noted:
- 2D drag-and-drop layout planner: tank size, substrate, hardscape, plants; grids, guidelines, layers, templates, layer editor.
- v3.3: share a layout by link and co-edit; auto shopping list of hardscape, plant and substrate quantities with PDF export; community gallery to browse and submit layouts.
- Per-plant info such as care level, CO2 need and pots needed per species (per commenters: https://www.fishlore.com/aquariumfishforum/threads/jls-aquascaping-compendium.507888/).
- A roundup says the workflow is dimensions, then background and substrate, then stones, wood, plants; it mentions ordering products through partners, limited to some regions (https://windowsreport.com/best-aquarium-design-software/, via search summary).

## What users love
- "Great for getting a general idea started" on a planned layout (MattW, Jan 2024): https://fishforums.net/posts/4308645
- Wider wood and hardscape choice than expected; "more options for wood than I thought" (Seisage, Jan 2024): https://fishforums.net/posts/4308645
- Used enough that a Fishlore hobbyist built a tips compendium from its screenshots, crediting it: https://www.fishlore.com/aquariumfishforum/threads/jls-aquascaping-compendium.507888/
- Free, fast ("a matter of minutes"), per its own pitch: https://scape-it.io/

## Complaints and weak spots
- Limited plant list; biotope plants like coontail and Sagittaria subulata missing (elephantnose3334, Jan 2024): https://fishforums.net/posts/4308645
- Users substitute look-alike plants and relabel them in an image editor (MattW): https://fishforums.net/posts/4308645
- Few natural decor items (botanicals, leaf litter, sticks) for South American-style scapes (Seisage, partly retracted for wood): https://fishforums.net/posts/4308645
- A roundup says it may lack the depth of dedicated design software and its partner ordering covers only some regions: https://windowsreport.com/best-aquarium-design-software/ (via search summary)
- One Fishlore commenter wanted CO2 needs flagged more clearly (the site does list them): https://www.fishlore.com/aquariumfishforum/threads/jls-aquascaping-compendium.507888/ (via search summary)
- Note: the feedback is from Jan 2024, before v3 and the shopping list; it may be partly out of date.
- Several pages (UKAPS, Fishlore, Windows Report) returned 403 to fetch, so those items rest on search summaries.

## What users wish it had
- More plant species, especially for biotopes; botanicals and more natural decor (implied, not explicit wishes): https://fishforums.net/posts/4308645
- Nothing else found.

## vs YourArium
- They have, we lack: shareable layout link with real-time co-editing; community gallery of submitted layouts; PDF shopping list sized by plant pots / substrate bags (we have a priced list but check quantities per plant); layers, grids and guidelines for precise 2D composition; an established community (Discord, YouTube partners).
- We have, they lack: real 3D orbit view, animated fish, stocking engine and compatibility gates, marine/brackish/vivarium worlds, real tank catalogue, brand-accurate gear, real prices and deals, guided setup, A/B compare, no-account use. Scape It appears to be hardscape and plant layout only.
- Ideas worth taking (ranked):
  1. Share-by-link (read-only first), plus a gallery of saved setups.
  2. Plants-per-species quantity ("pots needed") on the shopping list, if not already shown.
  3. Template layouts as one-click starting points per scape style.
  4. Plant needs shown inline (care level, CO2) as we plan; already partly done.
- Threat level: low. A free niche 2D scape planner with a small, loyal community; it covers the layout step only, not tank, stock or cost. Gallery and link sharing give it a social edge we lack.
