# 06. Behind Glass: Aquarium Simulator (Steam, Windows/macOS)
Researched 2026-11-25. Confidence: medium (Steam store, reviews and discussions read; no Reddit or forum threads about it turned up in searches; only ~10 negative reviews and ~10 recent ones sampled, not the full set).

## Snapshot
- URL: https://store.steampowered.com/app/1611580/ . Maker and publisher: BitBros Inc. Single-player. Windows 10 and macOS (Catalina+); a user also reports it runs on Linux Mint (https://steamcommunity.com/app/1611580/reviews/?browsefilter=mostrecent&filterLanguage=english&p=1).
- Price: $14.99 base; Complete Edition $19.97 with two DLC packs (Round Tank and Jellyfish, Decoration), each pack $4.99 (store page above). Steam Workshop, Steam Cloud, 14 languages.
- Status: active. Early Access began Aug 25, 2021, 1.0 on Jan 8, 2025 (store page). Steam announcements continued after 1.0: Apr 16, 2025 (fish, jellyfish, round tanks), Jul 8, 2025 (20 fish, recolor gravel), Nov 24, 2025 and Feb 16, 2026 (20 new species each), Jul 13, 2026 (New Fish Species and Decoration Pack DLC). Dates from the Steam events feed (https://store.steampowered.com/events/ajaxgetadjacentpartnerevents/?appid=1611580&count_before=0&count_after=8), so the maker is shipping roughly every 3 to 4 months, but the newest item is a paid DLC.
- Audience: Very Positive, 95% of about 392 reviews (store page); aggregators show 343 to 370 reviews depending on the date (https://store.steampowered.com/app/1611580). Discussion board has 269 threads (https://steamcommunity.com/app/1611580/discussions/). Small but healthy niche game.

## What it does
From https://store.steampowered.com/app/1611580/ :
- Freshwater and saltwater tanks; 280+ fish species plus crabs, turtles, axolotls; all decorations and plants unlocked from the start.
- Two modes: a water chemistry simulation (pH, salinity, plant, fish and algae growth, equipment) and a "designer mode" that drops the constraints for aesthetics.
- Fish breeding and a crossbreeding lab for hybrid morphs (https://www.thegamer.com/best-aquarium-simulator-games/ ).
- Steam Workshop content; background images (a forum thread asks where to get them).
- A third-party VR mod exists (Meta Quest 3, reported in a review at https://steamcommunity.com/app/1611580/reviews/?browsefilter=toprated&filterLanguage=english).

## What users love
- Visuals and fish animation: a 108-hour reviewer cites 4K HDR looks and says it beats a real tank without the upkeep; another praises animations and accurate hitboxes, water-quality and stress monitors, and the breeding lab (https://steamcommunity.com/app/1611580/reviews/?browsefilter=toprated&filterLanguage=english).
- Aquascape testing: one reviewer calls it a strong tool for trying setups before buying (same link). A longtime aquarist calls it a realistic representation of freshwater and reef tanks (same link).
- Relaxing, beginner friendly; some use it as "wall art" with the simulation turned off (https://steamcommunity.com/app/1611580/reviews/?browsefilter=mostrecent&filterLanguage=english&p=1).
- TheGamer lists it 8th among aquarium games, calling it "incredibly realistic" (https://www.thegamer.com/best-aquarium-simulator-games/ ).

## Complaints and weak spots
Negative reviews (https://steamcommunity.com/app/1611580/negativereviews/?browsefilter=toprated&filterLanguage=english), many from Early Access:
- Behavior: all species move alike; angelfish not aggressive, tetras not schooling or skittish; "screensaver or sandbox" rather than a simulator. Still repeated in a Nov 30, 2025 review (tetra motion unrealistic).
- Accuracy: no sexual dimorphism, some base colors wrong, tanks too small for large or saltwater species; fish die under a Walstad (heavy-planted) setup (May 2025 review).
- Missing sim depth: no algae control (Nov 2025), limited plant selection, tank can't cycle naturally, too many variables with no beginner baseline mode (Jun 2025).
- Game loop: no progression, tanks free in any size, fish don't grow, no store to sell bred fish (2022 review); one "Why don't the fish die?" thread (https://steamcommunity.com/app/1611580/discussions/).
- Bugs and performance: "Maximum Creature Complexity Reached" blocks adding fish (2022); Sim Mode freezes (2022); a recent review mentions turtle freezing; a thread requests an FPS limiter; Steam Deck and controller controls frustrating (https://steamcommunity.com/app/1611580/reviews/?browsefilter=mostrecent&filterLanguage=english&p=1).
- Maker confidence: one 39-hour reviewer (Oct 2024) said the dev had abandoned it; later updates contradict that.
- Hard to see fish in the default lighting (a recent reviewer, same recent-reviews link). One reviewer says it still falls short of the older AQUAZONE desktop aquarium.
- Monetization: new content now partly sold as DLC packs at $4.99.

## What users wish it had
All from the reviews links above and https://steamcommunity.com/app/1611580/discussions/ :
- Shrimp, snails, other inverts (the most repeated ask), plus seahorses, starfish, rays, octopus, lizards.
- More plants, control over plant size, realistic plant growth.
- Custom tank sizes, curved tanks, more substrates and 3D backgrounds, more music.
- Alternate skins and more colors and tail types for guppies, bettas, angelfish.
- Better species-specific behavior and a beginner mode; chemistry modules and campaign (reviewers expected them).

## vs YourArium
- They have, we lack: a living, animated tank you watch run over time (fish growth, breeding, hybrids/morph lab); water chemistry simulation and algae; 280+ species; crabs, turtles, axolotls (we have herps, check turtles/axolotls); Steam Workshop and VR mod community; offline desktop app.
- We have, they lack: real tank catalogue (their top asked-for feature is custom sizes) and real brand gear; stocking engine with compatibility gates and "outgrows it in N months"; priced shopping list and deals; guided setup; A/B compare; printable report; plants and aquascape styles with needs data; terrariums; free, no install, runs on phone.
- Ideas worth taking (ranked):
  1. Species-specific behavior (schooling tetras, aggressive angels): their loudest complaint and an easy way to look better than them.
  2. Shrimp and snails and richer plant roster: the most repeated wish; we are already planning plants and emersed growth.
  3. Plant growth over time and algae, already on our roadmap; reviewers explicitly miss it.
  4. A beginner baseline mode vs sandbox overload (maps to our experience gate).
  5. Lighting that makes fish easy to see; an FPS limiter option.
- Threat level: low. It is a game for relaxing and fantasy tanks, not for planning a real purchase; it lacks real products, prices and stocking verdicts, but it sets the realism bar for fish animation and may satisfy users who only want to watch.
