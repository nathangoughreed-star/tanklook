## What it does
All from https://thorleypark.com/aquascape:
- Pick freshwater (plants, tropical fish) or saltwater (corals, reef fish); the palette changes to match.
- Four tank sizes: Nano (~35 L), Standard (~110 L), Large (~240 L), XL Reef (~450 L).
- "Randomize" generates a fully stocked tank instantly.
- Manual build: drag in hardscape, plants, corals, fish, equipment and decor; choose a substrate and slope it; adjust each item's size, spin, tilt and height.
- 3D orbit/zoom view, with items kept inside the glass.
- Live care score, stocking meter and livestock list.
- Build report: stocking vs tank size, plus a care checklist (heater, lighting, filtration, flow, skimmer).
- Auto-saves in the browser; PNG export; save/load layout file.
- The page itself says stocking estimates are rough and it is a planning aid for inspiration.
