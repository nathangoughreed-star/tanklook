# 14. Biotope Aquarium Planner (iOS)
Researched 2026-11-25. Confidence: low (store page read; no user feedback found anywhere).

## Snapshot
- URL: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/biotope-aquarium-planner/id6787365200. Maker: Kursat Ufuk Coskun (individual developer, per the same page).
- Platform: iOS. Price: free with in-app purchases: Biotope Plus $4.99/month or $39.99/year, Remove Ads lifetime $4.99, credit packs $0.99 / $3.99 / $9.99 (same page). Age rating 16+.
- Status: version 1.0.1 (minor fixes), updated July 17 (year not shown); 1.0.0 shipped July 14 (same page). So it is a few months old and barely updated.
- Audience: the store page says it "hasn't received enough ratings or reviews to display an overview." No reviews shown. Searches for the name, the developer, Reddit and Product Hunt returned nothing about it (searched 2026-11-25). It is tiny.

## What it does
All from the App Store page above:
- AI diagnostics: upload a photo of algae or fish disease, get a diagnosis and treatment plan (this is probably what the credits pay for).
- Compatibility checks for fish, plants and invertebrates; region-based biotope guides.
- Water parameter logging (pH, ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, temperature), a chemistry simulator and drift warnings.
- Emergency response center; calendar with push reminders for maintenance.
- Aquascaping layout wizard (no detail on whether it is visual or a text wizard).
- Community feed for sharing setups.
- Covers freshwater, marine and biotope tanks.
