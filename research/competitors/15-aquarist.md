# 15. Aquarist (Steam, also consoles)
Researched 2026-11-25. Confidence: medium (store page and Steam top reviews read; two critic reviews; no Reddit/forum threads surfaced, Steam discussions not reached).

## Snapshot
- URL: https://store.steampowered.com/app/1430760/ . Maker: FreeMind S.A.; publisher FreeMind and PlayWay S.A. (same store page).
- A video game (aquarium-themed business sim), not a planning tool. Windows PC; also PS4/PS5/Switch/Xbox (https://3rd-strike.com/?p=168180).
- Price $15.99, 50% off to $7.99 at fetch; six paid DLCs (Arctic, Asian Fish, American Fish, Laboratory, Aquatic Supermarket, Japanese Garden); VR bundle (Steam page).
- Status: active. Early Access began March 8, 2022; 1.0 on March 29, 2024. Recent patch dates not shown on the page.
- Audience: Steam "Very Positive", 81% of 963 reviews positive (Steam page). OpenCritic 51 per a search summary (unverified). Critic scores: 6/10 (https://www.comfycozygaming.com/?p=7146), 6.7 (https://3rd-strike.com/?p=168180).

## What it does
From https://store.steampowered.com/app/1430760/ :
- Build freshwater and saltwater tanks, care for animals, take customer jobs.
- Run an aquarium shop and grow it into an oceanarium.
- Single player, Steam Cloud, Steam Deck listed, 27 languages.

## What users love
- The concept and the pet-keeping pull; top Steam review (133 helpful, 38.5 h) says it inspired buying a real fish: https://steamcommunity.com/app/1430760/reviews/?browsefilter=toprated&filterLanguage=english
- Building tanks to client specs and decorating; variety of fish and items: https://www.comfycozygaming.com/?p=7146
- Relaxing music, clear menus, customization, and some real fish-care and breeding info: https://3rd-strike.com/?p=168180
- Colorful visuals and relaxed aquarium building (common praise across top Steam reviews, same Steam link).

## Complaints and weak spots
All from the Steam top-reviews page above unless noted:
- Clunky decoration placement; items land on the glass; one review says it lacks aquascaping control (98 helpful).
- Bugs and stuck progress; Steam Deck "verified" label disputed (68 helpful).
- Long tutorial, timed days, little creative freedom, no sandbox mode (82 helpful).
- Not realistic: no real guidance on cycling, acclimation, water changes (46 helpful, 25.5 h, still recommended).
- Tedious side content (fossil hunting) and quests promoting the studio's other games.
- Confusing bundling with the studio's separate Aquatic Store Simulator, unclear DLC, repetitive grind: https://www.comfycozygaming.com/?p=7146
- Console port feels rough: finicky controls, six-item inventory, lifeless NPCs: https://3rd-strike.com/?p=168180

## What users wish it had
- A sandbox mode with free creativity (Steam review above).
- Better placement/aquascaping control (Steam reviews above).
- More realistic care mechanics (cycling, acclimation, water treatment) (Steam review above).
- Nothing found from Reddit or forums.
