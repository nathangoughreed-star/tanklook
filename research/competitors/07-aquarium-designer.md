# 07. Aquarium Designer (Steam, Windows)
Researched 2026-11-25. Confidence: medium (store page, two press reviews and a sample of Steam reviews found; no Reddit or forum threads surfaced).

## Snapshot
- URL: https://store.steampowered.com/app/1339000/ . Developer Sigur Studio; publishers Games Operators and PlayWay S.A. Released Oct 21, 2021.
- Platform: Windows only (Win 7+). Price $9.99; DLC Sea Life $4.99, Japan $3.99, Amazonia $3.99 (https://store.steampowered.com/app/1339000/).
- Audience: "Very Positive", 84% of about 1,100 Steam reviews positive (https://store.steampowered.com/app/1339000/). Steam page shows no update date; SteamDB patch-note mirrors exist but undated in search, and monthly content updates were reported (https://steamdb.info/patchnotes/7592535). SteamDB itself returned 403, so last-update date is unverified.
- It is a game, not a planning tool. Do not confuse with the same studio/publisher family's "Aquarist" (a separate game).

## What it does
All from https://store.steampowered.com/app/1339000/ unless noted:
- Career mode: design aquariums for clients (restaurants, hotels, offices, homes) from a brief; earnings unlock items.
- Pick tank shape and size, add filters, conditioners, air pumps, gravel, decorations, fish and plants.
- Casual mode (fish and plants cannot die) and Realistic mode (needs species conditions, simulates pH) (https://www.opencritic.com/game/12241/aquarium-designer).
- Free-build sandbox with few restrictions (https://www.gameindustry.com/?p=135799).
- Regular content updates adding fish, plants, decor and quests (https://steamdb.info/patchnotes/7592535).

## What users love
- Relaxing, good as ambient background viewing; creative freedom to build (Steam reviews: https://steamcommunity.com/app/1339000/reviews/).
- Sandbox is the highlight; fish and plants rendered with care, 4.5/5 for "prettiness" (https://www.gameindustry.com/?p=135799).
- Hobbyists like building freely without buying real equipment; one 143-hour reviewer values ongoing updates (https://steamcommunity.com/app/1339000/reviews/).
- Press: OpenCritic reviewer gave 70/100, saying it captures the feel of designing a tank (https://www.opencritic.com/game/12241/aquarium-designer).

## Complaints and weak spots
- Thin content: few plants, fish, decor; short campaign that ends abruptly; sandbox lacks goals (https://steamcommunity.com/app/1339000/reviews/; https://www.gameindustry.com/?p=135799).
- No shrimp or inverts, no nano fish; plants occupy fixed slots so no carpets or layering (https://www.gameindustry.com/?p=135799).
- Unrealistic fishkeeping: no overstocking penalty, fish don't grow, loose compatibility, filters/heaters matter little; reviewer says it is not a primer for real fishkeeping (https://www.gameindustry.com/?p=135799).
- Fish AI: species swim and school alike, bottom dwellers roam high; some wrong names and pH values that reviewers warn could mislead (https://steamcommunity.com/app/1339000/reviews/).
- Dated UI, no undo, hardscape can't be stacked; some crashes, lag, freezes (https://steamcommunity.com/app/1339000/reviews/; https://www.gameindustry.com/?p=135799).
- Timed missions clash with the relaxing tone; "wait for a sale" price comments (https://steamcommunity.com/app/1339000/reviews/).
- Setup costs far below real life and prices arbitrary (https://www.gameindustry.com/?p=135799).

## What users wish it had
- Shrimp/invertebrates, nano species, blackwater options, stackable hardscape, undo, more decor, goals in sandbox (https://steamcommunity.com/app/1339000/reviews/; https://www.gameindustry.com/?p=135799).
- Equipment that actually affects fish health; stocking and growth consequences (https://www.gameindustry.com/?p=135799).

## vs YourArium
- They have, we lack: a career/client-brief game loop and achievements; a relaxing "just watch it" feel with music; DLC themed packs (Japan, Amazonia, Sea Life). Native Windows install (offline).
- We have, they lack: honest calibrated stocking with growth ("outgrows it in N months") and compatibility gates; real catalogue tanks and brand gear; real prices and deals; undo/redo; inverts, herps, brackish, marine/reef; web and phone access, free, no install; compare and printable report.
- Ideas worth taking (ranked):
  1. Ambient "watch mode" (hide UI, calm music) since reviewers praise relaxing viewing.
  2. Species-specific swim and schooling behaviour; their reviewers call uniform AI a top flaw, so keep ours distinct.
  3. Sandbox "goals" or challenges (a client-brief style optional challenge) to give replay value.
  4. Stackable hardscape and plant layering/carpets, which they lack and users ask for.
- Threat level: low. Cheap casual game with a different audience (play, not planning), shallow fishkeeping model, Windows-only; useful mainly as proof that people want a relaxing tank-building experience.
