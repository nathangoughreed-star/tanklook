# 17. AqAdvisor (web)
Researched 2026-11-25. Confidence: medium-low (product well described; user feedback is a handful of forum threads, no store ratings, Product Hunt or YouTube found; Reddit thread seen only through search snippets).

## Snapshot
- URL: https://aqadvisor.com/ (saltwater version linked from it). Maker: Yeo-Hoon Bae, named on https://www.aqadvisor.com/articles/AqAdvisorIntro.php (the home page names no developer).
- Platform: browser only, no app mentioned. Price: free. Revenue: Amazon affiliate links; visitors are asked to shop through them to cover server costs, no display ads (https://aqadvisor.com/).
- Status: long-lived; the intro page is dated April 22, 2010 and describes weekly releases, which may no longer hold (https://www.aqadvisor.com/articles/AqAdvisorIntro.php). No current update date shown on the home page.
- Audience: no ratings or counts available. It is the de facto name that forum members cite when asked about stocking calculators; threads exist on FishForums, Aquarium Co-Op and r/Aquariums.

## What it does
From https://aqadvisor.com/ and the intro page:
- Freshwater tropical stocking calculator: enter tank dimensions, pick up to two filters, add species from a database of 917 species (intro page says 850+ species, 300+ filters, 40+ attributes per species).
- Reports stocking percentage, filtration adequacy, and compatibility (size, mouth size, aggression, water parameters); explicitly not an inch-per-gallon rule.
- Juvenile-size mode, search/advanced search, unit switching (cm, litres, F), preset examples, copy/paste report, bookmarkable URL.
- Disclaimers: does not guarantee long-term compatibility, recommends understocking for beginners, "far from" perfect, rarer species have less data, diet is stored but not analyzed, biotope checks only cover mbuna, and users are asked to report wrong results (intro page).

## What users love
- A retired FishForums moderator says it has improved over the years and is useful for planning a new tank, comparing it to a sat nav: https://www.fishforums.net/threads/how-accurate-is-aqadvisor.471174 (April 2021, 5 replies).
- A FishForums member finds it handy for planning; staying under 100% generally avoids overstocking (same search summary, FishForums "should AqAdvisor be stocked to 100" thread, not opened: https://www.fishforums.net/threads/should-aqadvisor-be-stocked-to-100.488320).
- A blog calls it not 100% accurate but good for newcomers: https://weseaquariumfish.blogspot.com/2021/07/is-aqadvisor-accurate.html (low-weight source).

## Complaints and weak spots
- Doubted percentages: one member entered 10 neon tetras in 10 gallons, got 80%, and called it "completely hocus-pocus" (https://www.fishforums.net/threads/how-accurate-is-aqadvisor.471174). Same thread: a 55-gallon mix scored 99%, and adding 8 more corydoras made 118%, and the poster questioned whether that was really too much.
- Non-linear, hard-to-explain scores: reported on Aquarium Co-Op, 28 chili rasboras = 100% in 15 gallons but 8 = 46% (https://forum.aquariumcoop.com/topic/30106-aqadvisor-makes-me-smile/; page returned 403 to me, figures come from search snippets only).
- Ignores live plants: a r/Aquariums commenter (Aug 2024 weekly question thread) says it does not account for plants and shows effectively 0% filtration with no filter. Seen via a search snippet only; the mirror link would not load: https://redlib.snake-cloud.ts.net/r/Aquariums/comments/1ekehux/autopost_weekly_question_thread_ask_raquariums/lhr48mh/?context=3 (original reddit URL not found).
- Veterans say stocking numbers and volume are only one factor and cannot all be programmed (FishForums thread above).
- Dated look, per one low-quality review page (https://bartists.info/@keira246935044?page=about); weak source.
- Self-stated gaps: rare species data, diet not analyzed, biotope check mbuna only (intro page).
- Freshwater tropical only; no visual, no tank view, no shopping list, no plants or hardscape.

## What users wish it had
- Live-plant awareness for filtration and bioload (r/Aquariums comment above; low-quality review page says the same).
- Explainable scores rather than unexplained percentages (inferred from the 80% and non-linear complaints above; no explicit feature request found).
- No explicit "I wish" threads found beyond that.
- Alternatives named in search results: App-aquatic (https://alternativeto.net/software/app-aquatic/), TankBud (freemium, community stocking calculator), AquaGenius (low-reliability source). No Reddit recommendation thread found.

## vs YourArium
- They have, we lack: a 15-year reputation and name recognition; the huge freshwater species database (917); explicit filter-model database (300+); a simple "paste in a report" flow; forum citability.
- We have, they lack: the 3D tank, visual layout, plants/hardscape, marine/brackish/herps, space and filtration split, "fine now, outgrows in N months", gates, prices and deals, guided setup, compare, printable report. Plants are the specific gap users complain about.
- Ideas worth taking (ranked):
  1. Explain every stocking percentage ("why 80%?"): the top trust complaint, and our honest-numbers pitch. Show the breakdown per species.
  2. Show live-plant effect on filtration/bioload visibly, since AqAdvisor ignores it.
  3. Offer an "AqAdvisor-style" quick report / import so AqAdvisor users can paste their stock list and see it in 3D.
  4. Keep the calibration against AqAdvisor (research/aq-compare.md) but publish where we differ and why.
  5. Link/cite species sources to pass the "forum veteran" sniff test.
- Threat level: low-medium. Dominant as a utility people already cite, but dated, freshwater only, with no visuals; a different job from ours, and forum veterans themselves treat it as rough.
