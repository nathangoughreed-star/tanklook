## What it does
From https://www.tankmates.io/ and https://www.tankmates.io/planner/ :
- Tank Planner: 3D planner where you add fish, plants, stones, wood and equipment and drag them into place; checks that fish get along, prices the tank with local shop prices, makes a shareable blueprint. 111 ready-made tank templates.
- Tank Mates checker and compare up to 4 species side by side.
- Encyclopedia: 932 species and 396 plants (browse section says 392), care library, guides, printables.
- Fish identifier from a photo, "Is my fish sick?" symptom checker, "Ask Finn" AI helper.
- Games and XP: daily tank-mate quiz, fish-vs-fish, flashcards.
- Pond planner and filtration calculator, koi/goldfish guides, reptile enclosure and health checks.
- Design Studio (posters, mugs, t-shirts), asset library, local shop listings.
- Freshwater focus; no reef or marine planner mentioned.
