# 18. My Aquarium Guide (iPhone app; store name now "Aquarium Guide Fish Identifier")
Researched 2026-11-25. Confidence: medium (many App Store reviews found via aggregator; no Reddit, forum, YouTube or Google Play feedback found).

## Snapshot
- URL: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id662602456 . Maker: Tayson Nguyen. Category Reference. Store title has changed several times (Aquarium Planner & Fish Guide, Aquarium Guide Fish Tank Rules, now Aquarium Guide Fish Identifier, subtitle "Tank Planner & Compatibility"): https://apps.appfollow.io/ios/aquarium-planner-fish-guide/662602456?country=us
- Platform: iOS (iPhone; Mac/Vision compatible). No Android listing found (search returned none): https://appadvice.com/app/my-aquarium-guide/662602456
- Model: free + in-app purchases. Plants Pack $2.99, African Cichlids Pack $2.99, Saltwater Fish Pack $2.99, Exotic Fish Pack $0.99, "Pro" at $7.99 / $29.99 / $44.99 (earlier listings showed Pro at $19.99-$22.99): https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id662602456
- Status: active. Version 26.8, Oct 30 (year not shown), notes "UI improvements, bug fixes": same page.
- Audience: 4.2 stars from about 14K ratings (same page). Reviews date from 2016 to 2023 on the sample I saw.

## What it does
From https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id662602456:
- Build freshwater or saltwater tanks; starter fish suggestions.
- Compatibility and stocking (overcrowding) warnings.
- 1,000+ fish profiles: temperament, size, diet, pH, temperature, origin; works offline (per https://apps.apple.com/np/app/my-aquarium-guide/id662602456).
- Identify likely fish from a camera or saved photo.
- Save, update and share tank setups.
- Pro unlocks more fish, plants and tanks.

## What users love
All from the review list at https://justuseapp.com/en/app/662602456/my-aquarium-guide/reviews unless noted:
- Compatibility checks: one says it stops you putting incompatible species together; another that it "keeps you in check".
- Beginner friendly, clear and well organized (several reviews).
- pH and temperature data praised (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id662602456).
- One reviewer found aggression and jumping-fish advice "pretty accurate".
- Helps plan "what our next fish are" for a first aquarium.

## Complaints and weak spots
- Small fish list; popular species missing, even after paying ("half of my freshwater fish aren't even listed"): https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id662602456
- Stocking math judged inaccurate: "DO NOT use it to calculate stocking info"; gallons required "seems off"; goldfish and Jack Dempsey tank sizes called wrong; stocking ignores that species differ in waste. A self-described professional aquarist flagged incompatible pairings. Source: https://justuseapp.com/en/app/662602456/my-aquarium-guide/reviews
- Paywalls: content sold as packs, plants paid, and a reviewer said the store text did not mention in-app purchases (same source).
- Very basic info; size shown only as S/M/L; no size measurements (same source).
- UX: cannot delete a tank without reinstalling, text needs scrolling, overly sensitive tank-size slider, no search or custom fish list (same source).
- The review-analysis site's own tally is 66.7% negative among 23 reviews, despite the 4.2 store score (same source; small sample, aggregator's own analysis).

## What users wish it had
All from https://justuseapp.com/en/app/662602456/my-aquarium-guide/reviews:
- More species (koi, popular fish), a bigger list.
- Substrate and decor options; a tank simulator; seeing fish movement.
- Juvenile vs adult size stages.
- A maintenance schedule.
- Template-based matching, search, custom fish lists, deleting tanks.

## vs YourArium
- They have, we lack: camera/photo fish identification; offline use; native iOS app store presence; an established base (14K ratings); 1,000+ species list (check our species count; reviewers say theirs is thin anyway).
- We have, they lack: any visual tank (3D, plants, hardscape, gear), real tank catalogue and custom sizes, a calibrated stocking engine with growth ("outgrows it in N months"), group sizes and sex ratios, equipment and prices, herps, brackish and reef, compare, report, no paywalls.
- Ideas worth taking (ranked):
  1. Photo "what fish is this" identifier (their one distinct feature; cheap on-device or API).
  2. Juvenile vs adult size display (our engine has growth; surface it per species).
  3. Per-species waste/bioload explained next to stocking numbers, since users distrust crude gallons-per-fish rules.
  4. Make sure deleting setups and searching species are obvious.
  5. Keep everything free; their paywall on species and plants is the loudest complaint.
- Threat level: low-medium. Large iOS audience and a simple, beginner-friendly checker, but a thin database, distrusted stocking numbers and no visuals leave our whole value proposition unmatched; the risk is only that it owns the iPhone App Store search slot.
