# 05. Tankfolio (web)
Researched 2026-11-25. Confidence: low (product found and described from its own pages; no real user feedback found anywhere).

## Snapshot
- URL: https://www.tankfolio.com/ (builder at https://www.tankfolio.com/tank-builder). Maker: not identified; the page shows only a 2026 copyright notice.
- Platform: browser. Price: not stated; no sign-in needed to model a tank, an account is needed to save builds and share them (https://www.tankfolio.com/tank-builder).
- Status: labelled "beta", "work in progress" (https://www.tankfolio.com/tank-builder). No last-update date shown.
- Audience: no Reddit, forum, Product Hunt, YouTube or app-store feedback found in several searches. The only third-party mention is an AI-generated-looking roundup ranking it 9th of 10 aquarium design tools, 6.8/10 (https://wifitalents.com/best/aquarium-design-software/); a second roundup lists 7.0/10 (https://worldmetrics.org/best/aquarium-design-software/). Treat these ratings as low-value; they are not user reviews.

## What it does
All from https://www.tankfolio.com/tank-builder unless noted:
- Enter tank dimensions in cm or inches; shows volume in litres and US gallons. Shapes: rectangle, long, tall, cylinder, bow-front.
- 3D preview of the tank outline, substrate options and fish movement styles (not a hardscape/plant layout editor on this page, though the roundup claims rock/plant placement; unverified).
- Freshwater species database: 62 species grouped into hubs (tetra, corydoras, betta, cichlid), each with a care page (e.g. https://www.tankfolio.com/fish/freshwater/barb/rosy-barb).
- Stocking estimate: adult length converted to total inches vs gallons (inch-per-gallon rule), labelled understocked / within range / overstocked. Schooling and tank-region settings only affect the preview, not the estimate.
- Homepage mentions compatibility warnings and group-size guidance, saved setups, and planned water-spec tracking and dosing reminders (https://tankfolio.com).

## What users love
Nothing found. No sourced user praise exists that I could locate.

## Complaints and weak spots
No user complaints found. Weak spots from its own pages and the roundup:
- Stocking uses the inch-per-gallon rule; the site itself says filtration, maintenance, behaviour and tank shape are not accounted for (https://www.tankfolio.com/tank-builder).
- Freshwater only; 62 species.
- Roundup says it does not cover bioload and water-parameter workflows end to end, and modelling depth is limited (https://wifitalents.com/best/aquarium-design-software/).
- Beta; no maker, pricing or dates disclosed.

## What users wish it had
Nothing found. The maker's own roadmap lists water-spec tracking and dosing reminders (https://tankfolio.com).
