# 08. Aquabuildr (iOS and Android app; the brief said "Web", but no web tool was found)
Researched 2026-11-25. Confidence: low-medium (product and App Store page read directly; only 4 written store reviews; no Reddit, forum or Product Hunt discussion found).

## Snapshot
- URL: https://aquabuildr.com/ . Maker: Futrix, L.L.C (developer shown on the App Store page): https://apps.apple.com/us/app/aquabuildr/id1568234361
- Platform: iOS and Android apps (site links both stores). Model: free first tank, Premium $4.99/month or $39.99/year (https://aquabuildr.com/ and the App Store page above).
- Status: very active. Version 4.12.12 on Nov 19 (2026 by sequence); roughly monthly releases since Dec 2024 (App Store version history, same URL).
- Audience: App Store US 4.3 stars from 274 ratings (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/aquabuildr/id1568234361). An earlier search snippet showed 4.0 from 19 ratings, probably a stale or other-country listing. No download counts, testimonials or user counts on its site.

## What it does
From https://aquabuildr.com/ unless noted:
- Compatibility engine: checks a fish against tank mates, temperature, pH, hardness, minimum tank size, temperament, group size; "vetted tank-mate lists" per species. The store listing claims a "patent-pending" compatibility algorithm with warnings and suggested corrections (https://apps.apple.com/md/app/aquabuildr/id1568234361).
- Tank Buildr: plan before buying; capacity readout warns at 80% and blocks at 100%.
- Tank Health Score (0-100) from ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, pH and water-change schedule.
- Logging: 14 water parameters, water changes, dosing, tasks, reminders, data export.
- Filter Recommendr: eight questions, 9 styles, 3 budget tiers, 1-210 gal.
- Aquabot AI assistant, fish database, Fish Trackr, community discussions and social feed, 21 languages.
- Saltwater support added Aug 2025 (v4.6); 100 fish added Jun 2025 (v4.4) (App Store version history).

## What users love
Only four written reviews are shown (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/aquabuildr/id1568234361):
- One user said it replaced their spreadsheet (Sep 2024).
- Design praised (Sep 2025 review).
- Concept liked (Apr 2024).
- A search summary reported a newcomer praising fast setup (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/aquabuildr/id1568234361).
- Developer replies to reviews and ships frequently.

## Complaints and weak spots
- Database accuracy and stocking calculations criticised, plus single filter/heater limit and subscription needed for multiple tanks (Sep 2025 review, https://apps.apple.com/us/app/aquabuildr/id1568234361).
- Task reminders not sending notifications; due dates not resetting (Nov 2025 review, same URL).
- Crash on creating a new tank reported in UK store reviews (per search summary of https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/aquabuildr/id1568234361; not verified directly).
- Paywall: extra tanks lock 30 days after Premium lapses (https://aquabuildr.com/).
- Not independent: no outside accuracy check of its compatibility claims found.

## What users wish it had
- More fish species and customisation, editing tanks and photos (Apr 2024; dev said v3.0 added these).
- Import/export (Sep 2024; dev said planned, export arrived v4.12).
- Multiple filters/heaters per tank (fixed in v4.8 "multiple equipment of the same type").
All from https://apps.apple.com/us/app/aquabuildr/id1568234361.

## vs YourArium
- They have, we lack: native mobile apps; water-parameter logging, health score, reminders and tasks (we deliberately skip care/upkeep); community, social feed and Fish Trackr; AI assistant; filter-recommendation wizard; 21 languages; accounts and cloud sync.
- We have, they lack: any visual 3D tank, scape and layout design, real brand gear drawn true, herps and terrariums, brackish, catalogue of real tanks, priced shopping list and deal sweeps, calibrated space/filtration stocking with outgrow forecast, split compare, printable report, guided setup, no paywall.
- Ideas worth taking (ranked):
  1. Capacity meter with explicit 80% warn / 100% block thresholds, if our UI lacks a clear one.
  2. A short "which filter fits this tank" questionnaire as a gear entry point.
  3. Show fish-database coverage count and fix reports ("report wrong data") since accuracy is their main complaint.
  4. Export/import of a setup file (users asked for it).
- Threat level: low-medium. Active, monetised, retention-focused logging app with a stocking checker; modest rating volume and no visual design, so it competes for the same newcomer's phone, not for the design/plan use case.
