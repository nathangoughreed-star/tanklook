## What it does
All from https://aquacompat.com/ and https://aquacompat.com/tools/stocking-calculator/.
- Stocking calculator: tank volume (US gal or litres) plus species and counts. The dropdown lists 29 species (the home page says 22 freshwater fish, shrimp and snails; counts differ between pages).
- Five checks: per-species minimum tank size, minimum group size, pairwise compatibility verdict, shared temperature and pH range, stocking load (adult inches per gallon plus estimated fish mass where data exists).
- Deliberately shows evidence instead of a single pass/fail percentage; says inches-per-gallon is a rough guide. States it does not model filters, plants or feeding and treats fish as adult size.
- Compatibility checker: 231 scored pairs, 1-10 score from six factors (temperature, temperament, size/predation, pH, hardness, swim level); 8+ compatible, 5-7 caution, under 5 incompatible; two hard deal-breakers (no shared temperature, or one eats the other). Method on /methodology/.
- Cites 23 documents from 12 publishers (retailers, FishBase, university extension).
- Other content: running-cost (electricity) calculator, CSV data library, printable charts, diagram library, tank-mate guides, 10-gallon community setup, species index, comparisons, nitrogen-cycle and fishless-cycling guides, new tank syndrome, equipment buying guides.
