## What it does
From https://impossibleaquariums.com/aquarium-planner and https://impossibleaquariums.com :
- "Ecological calculator": you enter tank size (4 to 120 in long, 4 to 48 in wide/high), GH/KH/pH, temperature, heater, filter, water movement, light (PAR or watts), CO2, photoperiod, substrate and rock/wood.
- Returns animal and plant recommendations with a reason and source for each verdict, checked against published ranges.
- Stocking estimate, shared-water summary, and a cycle and stocking-order schedule.
- Export: copy as text, copy a link, print, email via Formspree, or "Aquarium AI" (builds a Tank Profile to paste into a chatbot).
- Form to request missing species.
- Separate free Aquarium Calculator (volume, water weight, substrate).
- Tied to the author's SLESS substrate method (four mineral layers) and its shop kits.
- Limits: freshwater only; brackish promised "with the next data update", no date. Page says it is for planning only and does not predict results.
