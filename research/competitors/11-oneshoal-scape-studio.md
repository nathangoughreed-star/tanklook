## Snapshot
- URL: https://oneshoal.com/scape-studio. Maker: OneShoal.com, operated by DWH Venture Works Limited, a UK-based aquarium/pond marketplace: https://oneshoal.com
- Platform: browser, needs a OneShoal account. Price: "free to use with a OneShoal account", no card for the Free tier; setup allowances and optional paid tools sit on a pricing page (https://oneshoal.com/scape-studio).
- Status: labelled Beta. No launch or update dates anywhere on the pages (only a 2026 copyright notice): https://oneshoal.com
- Audience: not stated. The site shows "No local listings yet in United States", so the marketplace is UK-centric and thin in the US: https://oneshoal.com
- Web searches for "OneShoal" (reviews, Reddit, forums, Product Hunt) returned no relevant results. No ratings, threads or comments found.

## What it does
From https://oneshoal.com/scape-studio and https://oneshoal.com:
- Plan a tropical tank, marine reef or garden pond, starting from an example or a blank canvas.
- Drag, drop, turn and stack plants, rocks, wood and similar items.
- 3D preview with lighting and water effects; switch between plan and preview.
- Designs private by default, saved to the account ("My Shoal"); optional view-only sharing.
- The page says a design is a planning aid, not a stocking calculation or livestock assessment.
- Surrounding platform (separate from Scape Studio): marketplace for livestock and gear, tank/livestock/water-reading logs, species guides, a compatibility checker for up to three species, local "Shoal Atlas", shop tools (https://oneshoal.com).
