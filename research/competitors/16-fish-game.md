# 16. Fish Game (Steam, Windows)
Researched 2026-11-25. Confidence: medium (Steam page, Steam reviews page, discussions, one press review and Steambase found; no Reddit or forum threads surfaced).

## Snapshot
- URL: https://store.steampowered.com/app/1372150/Fish_Game/ . Maker: A Shell in the Pit Games (with Creative Ink Games), same page.
- Platform: Windows 64-bit PC, Steam Deck supported. Price $24.99 list, $14.99 on the Nov 2026 sale; paid DLC packs exist (fish packs, substrate packs), e.g. https://store.steampowered.com/app/3537170/Fish_Game__Indian_Subcontinent_Fish_Pack_1/
- Released Oct 26, 2023 (full release, but see complaints on Early Access labeling). Active: weekly community aquascaping challenge posts through Nov 22, 2026, and a "Plant Update" devlog on Nov 5, 2026 covering ferts and floater roots: https://store.steampowered.com/feeds/news/app/1372150/
- Audience: 607 Steam reviews, 92% positive, Very Positive (store page above). Steambase shows about 659 reviews, all-time peak 168 concurrent (Oct 2023), a June 2026 monthly peak of 21 and 6 playing at fetch time: https://steambase.io/games/fish-game/steam-charts . Small but loyal niche.

## What it does
From https://store.steampowered.com/app/1372150/Fish_Game/ :
- Freshwater aquarium simulator: 130+ fish species with personalities and evolving relationships; schooling, chasing, fighting, carnivory.
- 50 plant species that propagate; 50+ rock, driftwood and hardscape types; 25 sculptable, layerable substrates.
- Hunger and 11 food types; simulated CO2, ammonia and oxygen.
- Tanks from 7 to 3,000 gallons, XP-unlocked content, a shop with no currency.
- "No Death Mode", photo mode with dolly shots and community contests.
- Planned: advanced water chemistry and equipment, advanced plant needs, "Aquadex", breeding with genetics and mutations.

## What users love
- Realistic fish behavior, needs and health, and visible live status of what fish are doing; no microtransactions in the base game; responsive developers and Discord; looks good and runs on modest hardware. Source: top-rated Steam reviews, https://steamcommunity.com/app/1372150/reviews/?browsefilter=toprated&filterLanguage=english
- Family-friendly and a stand-in for owning a real tank (same source).
- Press: fish "look and behave like their real species", passionate devs, graphics praised (reviewer scored it 5): https://www.comfycozygaming.com/2023/10/29/fish-game-review/
- The store page carries creator praise such as "Greatest Aquarium Game Ever Made?" (https://store.steampowered.com/app/1372150/Fish_Game/).

## Complaints and weak spots
- Missing livestock: no saltwater, shrimp, snails or other inverts; the most repeated ask (Steam top reviews, link above).
- Water systems feel thin: oxygen confusing, plants oxygenate too little, no bubbler or filter, temperature missing, chemistry "lackluster" (same reviews). A discussion thread asks how temperature and nitrogen work: https://steamcommunity.com/app/1372150/discussions/
- Controls and UI: plant trimming hard to control, fish stats need too many menu clicks, no undo, no deleting or duplicating tanks or fish (Steam top reviews).
- Bugs: stretched fish, black shadows, z-fighting, missing textures (Steam reviews); discussion threads report a random camera switching, 4K issues, lost save profiles and a vanished tank (discussions link above).
- At launch the press review found only male fish in shops so no breeding, injured fish could not be healed, XP stopped counting until restart, algae returned fast, and the game felt like an "interactive background" with limited mechanics; it called $24.99 not worth full price: https://www.comfycozygaming.com/2023/10/29/fish-game-review/
- Early Access label confusion: the same review says the in-game build showed Early Access while Steam showed full release.
- Sandbox only, no consequences mode (Steam top reviews). Players ask whether it is an idle game (discussions).
- Declining player count: monthly peaks in the 20s in 2026 (Steambase link above).

## What users wish it had
- Breeding, automated feeding and water changes, buying and selling fish, a mode with stakes (Steam top reviews).
- More species: jellyfish, frogs, sharks, deep sea; compatibility tips for fish and plants; achievements (same).
- Wallpaper or second-monitor mode; filter sound without music (same).
- Reorder tanks, an aggression-off setting, an updated store guide (discussions link above).

## vs YourArium
- They have, we lack: real fish behavior sim (schooling, fighting, hunger, carnivory) running live; gameplay loop with XP; a plant propagation sim; photo mode and weekly community challenges; tanks up to 3,000 gallons; Steam Deck and desktop-app presence.
- We have, they lack: saltwater/reef, brackish, terrariums and herps; inverts; real tank and gear catalogue with brand colours; stocking engine with compatibility gates and "outgrows it" warnings; shopping list with prices and deals; guided setup; A/B compare; printable report; web and phone with no install; undo/redo and multi-select (which Fish Game users ask for).
- Ideas worth taking (ranked):
  1. Undo/redo, delete and duplicate for tanks and animals: Fish Game users cite their absence (we already have undo; keep it prominent).
  2. Visible live "what is this fish doing / feeling" status on tap, which users praise.
  3. Photo mode with a camera dolly, and a themed weekly scape challenge for community pull.
  4. Visible equipment effects (bubbler, filter oxygen, temperature) so water feels simulated, if we ever model it.
  5. Wallpaper / ambient screen mode for a tank.
- Threat level: low. Windows-only, small and declining player base, freshwater only, a toy rather than a planning tool; it overlaps on looks and fish behavior, not on stocking advice or buying.
