# 12. Aquascaper Studio (web)
Researched 2026-11-25. Confidence: low (product found and described from its own site; no user feedback found anywhere).

## Snapshot
- URL: https://aquascaperstudio.com/ . Maker: a single developer, Nyein Chan Soe, aquascaping since 2019 (per the site).
- Platform: desktop/laptop browser with WebGL; phones open it but the layout is "still being tuned". No account, no download.
- Price: free. Status: active-looking (page copyright 2026); no changelog or "last updated" date shown.
- Audience: no reviews, Reddit threads, forum posts, Product Hunt or store entries found in several searches (name, domain, developer name). Very small, new, one-person project.
- Not to be confused with: the "Aquascaper" Blender add-on https://superhivemarket.com/products/aquascaper-aquarium-generator-for-blender , "Scaper - The aquascaping app" https://discussions.unity.com/t/scaper-the-aquascaping-app/896362 , or "Vivarium: Nature Design" https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6758380171 .

## What it does
All from https://aquascaperstudio.com/ :
- Low-poly 3D aquarium builder in the browser.
- Tank choice: rimless glass, framed, or bowl.
- Aquasoil or sand substrate, sculpted with a brush.
- Hardscape: dragon stone, lava rock, spiderwood, driftwood.
- Freshwater plants, mosses, carpet plants.
- Freshwater fish and shrimp that swim and behave by species.
- Live view of the finished tank. Site quotes about 42 fps on an Apple M1 in a large scene.
