# FS1: bala shark and red-tail shark (R77), reference notes

Models were matched side by side to Wikimedia Commons photos (not committed; viewed during the lane):
- Bala shark, "Balantiocheilos melanopterus.JPG" and "Haibarben (Balantiocheilos melanopterus).jpg": silver body with large dark-edged scales, big dark eye with a thin gold rim,
  tall raked dorsal, deeply forked tail whose lobes are black on both edges with a cream stripe inside, black pelvic and anal fins.
- Red-tail shark, "Epalzeorhynchos bicolor.jpg" and "Red-tailed black shark.jpg": velvet charcoal-black body, large swept triangular dorsal,
  bright red-orange forked tail, black pelvic and anal fins, small barbels at the mouth.
