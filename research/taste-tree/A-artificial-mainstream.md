# Taste tree, slice A: artificial, decorative, themed, mainstream and keeper-type-driven freshwater tanks

Researcher: slice A (siblings: B = natural planted / biotope / low-tech, C = marine / reef / brackish).
Date: 2026-10-30. Units: US (gallons, inches, deg F) with metric in brackets only where a source used it.

Conventions: "EST" marks my estimate (no hard data). Popularity = share of freshwater keepers who would recognise the look as "their kind of tank", ordinal: huge / large / medium / small / niche. Cost and care tiers are low / med / high. "Blends" lists nodes that visually merge with it. Source-quality note: most web sources found are hobby blogs, forums and retailer pages; treat specifics as folk practice, not measured data.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
## 0. Where slice A sits in the tree

Proposed Round 1 (broadest split, picture-driven):
1. "Looks like nature" (B and part of C) vs
2. "Looks like a fun / colourful / decorated thing" (A1) vs
3. "Looks like design / furniture" (A2) vs
4. "The animal IS the look" (A3, plus A4/A5 working tanks) vs
5. "Reef" (C).

Reason to add the "animal IS the look" branch at Round 1: a goldfish, flowerhorn, axolotl, oscar or discus keeper does not recognise themselves in "natural vs artificial"; the choice they care about is the animal. A picture of a big red fish in a bare black-backed tank, or a pale axolotl on sand, instantly reads as theirs. Without it these people (est. 15 to 25 % of keepers) fall in the cracks.

### Demographic data found (with sources)
- US households with freshwater fish: about 10 million; saltwater about 2 million (APPA 2025 Fish and Reptile Report). Trend is down: 12.5 million (2017-18 NPOS, per reefs.com) and 11.5 million (2019-20 NPOS, per Toledo Blade) to about 10 million now. https://americanpetproducts.org/news/from-bigger-tanks-to-stronger-bonds-fish-reptile-ownership-evolves-in-2025 and https://reefs.com/?p=173529
- 77 % of fish owners keep 2+ fish (up 7 % since 2018); single-fish ownership down 19 % since 2018. 50 % of fish owners have acrylic tanks (up 12 %). 70 % of freshwater owners use aquarium lighting (79 % saltwater). Same APPA page.
- Generation: Millennials are 38 % of freshwater owners (46 % of saltwater). Gen Z is 33 % of reptile owners. Same APPA page.
- Where bought: fish stores 36 % of freshwater owners (up 57 % since 2018); pet superstores 30 % of fish owners overall (down 14 % vs 2023). The big-box starter-kit entry path is eroding toward the local fish store (LFS), implying more "considered" setups. Same APPA page.
- Gifts: 44 % of freshwater owners bought a fish product as a gift (51 % overall). Same APPA page.
- Brazilian survey of 906 keepers (Facebook-group sample, not representative): 88 % male, 73 % aged 20 to 40, 69 % higher education; aesthetics the main motivation (76 % cited beauty of fish and aquariums); behaviour (26 %) and appearance (22 %) key in choosing species; 86 % freshwater only, 5 % marine only, 9 % both; 34 % kept mixed-species tanks. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9661971
- GloFish: company-claimed roughly 15 % share of aquarium fish sales (relayed by a hobby blog, unaudited); sold through superstores and mass merchants (Spectrum Brands filings). https://reefs.com/how-much-is-glofish-worth/
- No usable Reddit poll or APPA breakdown by "tank style" was found. All per-style shares in this file are EST. Use the APPA stats above as the anchor that mainstream, single-species and kit-based tanks dominate by household count while the hobby-forum aesthetic (planted, biotope) dominates by online visibility.

### A1. playful-decorative (parent: artificial-mainstream)
Shared look: saturated colour, visible ornaments, light-hearted. Splits into "glow", "scene", and "plain kit".

**1. glofish-neon-blacklight**
- Names / search terms: GloFish tank, neon tank, blacklight aquarium, fluorescent fish tank, UV tank, glow tank, electric green tetra tank.
- Parent: A1. Personas: kid-parent, teen, casual-decorator.
- Visual cues: black or dark gravel (sometimes with fluorescent flecks), fluorescent plastic plants and ornaments in pink, green, orange, blue; blue or purple LED / blacklight; dark room; animals in electric pink, green, orange, purple, blue, red. Water clear. Dense colour, little empty space.
- Representative tank: 10 to 20 gal rectangle, black GloFish gravel 1.5 in, three fluorescent plastic plants (pink, green, orange) at back-left and back-right, one fluorescent cave or castle centre-left, black background, blue LED strip in hood, 5 to 8 GloFish tetras / danios / a GloFish betta.

- Distinguisher: fish and decor glow (saturated fluorescent colour on near-black).
- Blends: colour-led-party, fantasy-scifi-glow, kid-first-tank-kit, rainbow-gravel-clown (opposite substrate mood).

## 5. Gaps / caveats
- No quantitative per-style popularity data exists publicly; all shares are EST. The APPA data supports only household counts, single vs multi-fish, purchase channel and generational mix.
- Source coverage of the Asian-display (flowerhorn, arowana) keepers is largely forum and blog; folk feng shui claims have no empirical support (placing colours / numbers like nine fish are tradition).
- Several web sources are SEO blogs; use for look descriptions only, not for care or numbers.
- Care and ethics: goldfish in bowls, bettas in 1 gal vases and overstocked rainbow kits are widespread but poor practice; the app may want to show welfare-informed variants (bigger tank, filter) in the same look instead of excluding these tastes.
