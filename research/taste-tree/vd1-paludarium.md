# VD1: Paludarium archetypes (6 families, 15 archetypes)

Researched 2026-10-30. Sources are hobby care sheets, retailer guides and forums (no peer-reviewed husbandry data found); figures are ranges, and where sources disagree both ends are shown. US units. Prior nodes consulted by id: B-natural-freshwater (paludarium, paludarium-waterfall-jungle, paludarium-rock-wall, paludarium-flooded-forest, riparium, half-tank-shoreline, wabi-kusa-bowl), C-marine-brackish-land (land-paludarium, brk-mangrove-riparium, brk-mudskipper-shore).

## 0. Decisions at a glance

| Family | Archetypes (final) | Notes |

Merges / cuts recommended: (a) riparium-garden is the same picture as half-tank "open-top-pothos-roots" in B (already merged there); (b) land-paludarium and paludarium-rock-wall (C / B) fold into rainforest-waterfall + cascade-wall; (c) brk-mangrove-riparium folds into mangrove-paludarium (cross-linked from brackish, one id); brk-mudskipper-shore = mudskipper-mudflat (one id, cross-linked); (d) half-tank-shoreline = riverbank-shore; vivarium-with-water-feature (mostly land, tiny pool) = cascade-wall when a drip wall is present, otherwise out of scope (dart-frog vivarium, not an aquatic picker item).

Safety flags (summary): turtle-shore (size, UVB, heat, adult growth); red-claw crab and mudskipper (must be brackish, escape artists); newt-shore (cool water, toxic skin, no mixing with fire-bellied toads, no heater/lamp); cypress-swamp (carnivorous plant water chemistry and dormancy; "cypress" is a tree that outgrows any tank so knees are props); mangrove (wild-collected propagules restricted, outgrows tank within about a year if untrimmed); riparium (open top = jumpers, pothos/peace lily are mildly toxic to pets, stems rot if kept wet); floating-island (raft shades and blocks surface gas exchange if it covers too much).

Mangrove-paludarium vs brackish mangrove-estuary aquarium (answer to the key question): the same plant, different land fraction. Aquarium version (brk-mangrove-riparium) is about 90-100 % water, propagule canopy through the open top, roots dangling underwater, no beach. Paludarium version is about 35-45 % land: a sloped mud-sand bank, fiddler crabs, a visible waterline through the roots. Picker should show the BANK. Brackish aquarium stays under Marine/Brackish; the paludarium tile lives here.

## 1. Answers to the key questions

- rainforest-waterfall vs cascade-wall: look-alike at thumbnail size unless defined by WATER FRACTION and MOTION. Rainforest-waterfall = one bold, audible plunge down a mossy rock face into a visible pond (water about 35-40 % of the floor, land and rock about 60 %). Cascade-wall = no plunge: the whole back wall (cork / foam / stone) weeps in thin sheets and drips over planted ledges into a small basin (water about 10-15 %, land about 80 %), bromeliads and ferns dominating, frog-vivarium feel. Keep both; if the picker's tile art cannot show that difference, merge into one "Rainforest Waterfall" and move the drip-wall look to a variant of cliff-gorge. Sources: gensou paludarium how-to describes pump-to-drip-wall builds; aquifarm lists "waterfall" and "emersed/no waterfall" as separate styles.
- turtle-shore: safe ONLY at 40 gal or larger for ONE adult musk turtle (sources range 20-55 gal; floor area beats depth; 36 x 18 x 16 in minimum footprint quoted). Sliders / cooters / painted turtles are NOT safe in any paludarium-sized build (rule of thumb about 10 gal per inch of shell; adult red-eared slider 60-125 gal). Needs UVB (5.0-10.0 tube, replace every 6-12 months), basking dock, filter rated about 2x volume, plants turtle-proofed. Gate the tile below 40 gal.
- newt-shore: right constraints are cool (60-68 F, brief 72 F; stress above 75-78 F), no heater unless the room is cold, mostly aquatic (water 60-70 % with a ramp / island), 20 gal long for a pair to four (30 x 12 x 12 in minimum quoted), no heat lamp.
- red-claw crab: brackish (SG 1.005-1.015; some sources say freshwater survives but does not thrive), tight lid, 10 gal quoted minimum for one but 20-30 gal honest.
- cypress-swamp: recognizable (dark tea water, buttress trunks, knees, Spanish moss, duckweed film) but the carnivorous plants are the problem: pitchers / flytraps want RO / rain water, acidic peat-sphagnum, 4-6+ h direct light and (Sarracenia, Dionaea) a 3-4 month cold dormancy, which a warm humid tank cannot give. Render them as an accent on a sphagnum hummock, flagged "needs separate low-mineral water and winter rest", not a core cue.
- mangrove-paludarium vs brackish aquarium: see section 0.

## 2. Archetypes

Format: density = open / balanced / full. Temperatures in F. Min = minimum honest tank in US gal.

### FAMILY 1: Rainforest Waterfalls

#### rainforest-waterfall - "Rainforest Waterfall"
- Tagline: "A jungle cliff that roars. Plan for the pump hum."
- Cues: single plunge over a mossy rock face; pond at the base with ripples; mist hanging in the air; broad-leaf aroids and ferns overhead.
- Contrast with cascade-wall: one loud waterfall into a real pond (water about 35-40 %) vs a weeping wall with only a small basin.
- Baseline: land/water about 60/40; hardscape = layered stone or foam rock face on the left third rising to the top; plants = pothos, philodendron, ferns, bromeliads, java moss on the rock; substrate = hydroton + gravel pond floor, soil planting zones; water feature = pump-fed waterfall; light mood = bright green top light, dim pond; open space = right half pond surface and a small beach.
- Density: open = single fall, 3 plant clusters / balanced = fall plus ledges, moss, fern canopy / full = leaf wall hiding the rock.
- Rendering: animated flowing water plane or particle sheet, splash foam ring, mist volume, rock face mesh, moss decals, glass condensation.
- Water: soft to medium, pH 6.5-7.5. Temp: 72-80 air, 70-78 water. Min: 20 gal tall (18 x 18 x 24 in about 34 gal is the practical floor with a pond and pump).
- Animals: white cloud mountain minnows or small tetras (cool end), shrimp, optional dart frogs only if land is built separate from the pond; fire-bellied newts at the cool end.
- Welfare: pump intake guard for fish and shrimp, lid with ventilation gap, humidity 70-100 %, never mix dart frogs and fish water unscreened; most frogs need a bioactive floor, not a pond.
- Sources: https://gensou.sg/how-to-create-paludarium-step-by-step/ ; https://aquifarm.com/paludarium-with-waterfall ; https://bantam.earth/?p=722

#### cascade-wall - "Drip Wall Jungle"
- Tagline: "A living wall that never stops dripping. Hose bib not included."
- Cues: back wall of cork / foam / stone covered in bromeliads and moss; thin sheet flow and drip lines; small basin at the base; land dominates.
- Contrast with rainforest-waterfall: no single plunge; land about 80 %; basin tiny; plants as vertical stripes.
- Baseline: land/water about 85/15; hardscape = cork slab or sculpted foam wall with stepped ledges; plants = bromeliads, Philodendron, ferns, Selaginella, creeping fig, mosses; substrate = ABG-style soil mix on a drainage layer; water feature = pump to header pipe, sheet flow down the wall; light mood = even green light, warm highlights on bromeliads; open space = the bare front of the lower third.
- Density: open = 2 ledges planted / balanced = 5 ledges with moss and pools / full = covered wall.
- Rendering: wet-surface shader with moving drip streaks, background panel mesh, hanging moss, droplet particles.
- Water: soft, pH 6-7. Temp: 70-80. Min: 20 gal tall (12 x 12 x 24 about 15 gal kits exist; fine for plants and frogs only).
- Animals: dart frogs are the iconic occupant but are a vivarium topic (humidity 70-100 %, no fish in the basin); invertebrate clean-up crew; basin may hold shrimp.
- Welfare: dart frogs need their own species guidance; keep the pump from reaching 82+ F; avoid standing basin deeper than the animal needs.
- Sources: https://gensou.sg/how-to-create-paludarium-step-by-step/ ; https://www.joshsfrogs.com/waterfall-paludarium-habitat-kit-12x12x24-exo-terra.html

### FAMILY 2: Overgrown Riverbanks

#### riverbank-shore - "Sandy Riverbank"
- Tagline: "A beach you can't walk on. Sand migrates."
- Cues: sand slope entering water; roughly half the tank shallow beach and low grass; stones on the bank; emersed grasses and moss on the land side.
- Contrast with flooded-forest: sunny open bank and open water vs submerged trunks and canopy.
- Baseline: land/water about 45/55; hardscape = driftwood root at the shoreline, few stones; plants = emersed grasses, Hydrocotyle, moss, Anubias on wood, a tuft of Vallisneria; substrate = fine sand beach graded to gravel; water feature = none (optional gentle drip); light mood = sunlit daylight, warm midday highlights; open space = water surface and beach front.
- Density: open = bare beach with one wood / balanced = grass fringe, driftwood, stones / full = overgrown bank into the water.
- Rendering: shoreline slope mesh with wetting line, sand ripple texture, waterline refraction, emersed grass sway.
- Water: soft to medium. Temp: 74-80. Min: 40 gal long (36 x 18 x 16 in) to leave a beach and open water.
- Animals: small tetras / rasboras, Corydoras, shrimp; beach for isopods / snails; keep amphibians as a separate archetype.
- Welfare: sand must be rinsed; slope retention with rocks; avoid a land bed without drainage (water table smell).
- Sources: B-natural-freshwater node half-tank-shoreline ; https://gensou.sg/?p=20092

#### flooded-forest - "Flooded Forest"
- Tagline: "Tree trunks standing in tea. Leaves keep falling."
- Cues: trunks and branches rising out of dark tea-stained water; leaf-litter floor; canopy leaves at the top; shoal of small tetras.
- Contrast with riverbank-shore: no beach, water about 75 %, tea color, woody verticals vs a sunny slope.
- Baseline: land/water about 20/80; hardscape = branchy driftwood trunks emerging through the surface, root tangle; plants = few, shade-tolerant (Cryptocoryne, Anubias, floating Salvinia, epiphyte moss on wood); substrate = fine sand and deep leaf litter (Indian almond, oak); water feature = none; light mood = dim green canopy light, amber water; open space = open midwater for the shoal.
- Density: open = 2 trunks, little litter / balanced = 4-5 trunks, litter, hanging leaves / full = root thicket.
- Rendering: tinted volumetric water, tannin gradient, leaf-litter instancing, trunk-water intersection foam, canopy leaf cards above the rim.
- Water: soft, acidic (pH 4.5-6.5 in builds; wild readings as low as 3.5-5.3). Temp: 75-82. Min: 40 gal (36 x 18 x 16 in) to hold a shoal.
- Animals: cardinal tetras, Corydoras pygmaeus / habrosus, Apistogramma, dwarf pencilfish.
- Welfare: schooling species in 8+; acid water needs stable buffering and no aggressive pH swings; wild-caught cardinal supply issues.
- Sources: https://gensou.sg/?p=41855 ; https://gensou.sg/?p=21887 ; https://biotopeaquariumproject.com/bam/igapo-upper-rio-negro-tributary-brazil-dtspeller/

### FAMILY 3: Swamps & Mangroves

#### cypress-swamp - "Cypress Swamp"
- Tagline: "Black water, knobbly knees, drooping moss. Humid year round."
- Cues: dark tea water covered with a duckweed film; trunk with a flared base and cypress knees; Spanish moss draping from branches; a hummock of sphagnum with a pitcher plant.
- Contrast with flooded-forest: temperate-Southern look (duckweed, knees, moss) vs tropical (leaf litter, canopy); water filmed green vs open amber.
- Baseline: land/water about 30/70; hardscape = trunk base with 4-6 sculpted knees, a log; plants = Taxodium bonsai or a faux trunk, Spanish moss (Tillandsia usneoides), duckweed (Lemna), sphagnum hummock with one Sarracenia or Drosera as accent, ferns; substrate = peat-sand mix on the hummock, dark mulm on the floor; water feature = none; light mood = misty dim light, golden shafts; open space = quiet open water lane.
- Density: open = 3 knees and a moss strand / balanced = knees, duckweed, hummock / full = thick moss and fern thicket.
- Rendering: cypress-knee prop set (instanced), draped moss strands, floating duckweed layer, volumetric mist, tea water.
- Water: soft, acidic (pH 5.5-6.8). Temp: 65-80 (US native species tolerate cool). Min: 29 gal; 40 gal preferred.
- Animals: native fish such as least killifish, flagfish, pygmy sunfish; eastern newt; mud turtle excluded (see turtle-shore); fiddler-free.
- Welfare: carnivorous plants require RO / rain water, 4-6+ h direct light, and winter dormancy for Sarracenia / Dionaea; tropical Nepenthes / Drosera better suit warm tanks; real cypress outgrows any tank (use dwarf or prop); Spanish moss rots if kept wet.
- Sources: https://hgic.clemson.edu/factsheet/creating-an-indoor-carnivorous-wetland-terrarium-a-step-by-step-guide/ ; https://mdc.mo.gov/magazines/xplor/2021-11/wild-cypress-swamp ; https://www.floridamuseum.ufl.edu/southflorida/habitats/cypress-swamps/flora-fauna/

#### mangrove-paludarium - "Mangrove Shore"
- Tagline: "Roots in brackish mud, crabs on parade. Salt creeps."
- Cues: red-mangrove seedlings with prop roots in water; sloping mud bank on one side; fiddler crabs; waterline cutting through the roots.
- Contrast with brackish mangrove aquarium (brk-mangrove-riparium): has a visible mud bank (land about 35-45 %) vs a fully filled tank; contrast with mudskipper-mudflat: roots and canopy dominate, mudflat is open and bare.
- Baseline: land/water about 40/60; hardscape = cork bark, prop-root tangle, rock stack; plants = Rhizophora mangle propagules (3), seagrass-like Halophila optional; substrate = muddy sand graded to bank; water feature = none; light mood = bright warm light, wet reflections; open space = shallow water in front of the bank.
- Density: open = 1 sapling / balanced = 3 saplings plus bank / full = root forest.
- Rendering: tidal waterline, wet-mud shader, prop-root meshes, small crab agents, salt-crust decals on glass.
- Water: brackish SG 1.005-1.015 (about 7-20 ppt); water depth 3-5 in on the bank side. Temp: 75-82. Min: 30 gal; 40 gal long preferred (propagules outgrow a tank in about a year).
- Animals: fiddler crabs, Atlantic / mangrove shrimp, mollies, bumblebee gobies.
- Welfare: lid on, land above waterline; trim saplings; source cultured propagules (wild collection restricted in many places); salt corrosion on equipment.
- Sources: https://gensou.sg/?p=29481 ; https://gensou.sg/?p=41854 ; https://www.furrycritter.com/pages/products_gear/fish/fiddler_crab_housing.htm

### FAMILY 4: Rocky Streams & Cliffs

#### boulder-creek - "Mossy Boulder Creek"
- Tagline: "A mountain creek in a box. Needs a strong pump."
- Cues: rounded river boulders and cobbles; whitewater riffles; moss on the stones; riparian grass spilling into the stream; low land.
- Contrast with cliff-gorge: horizontal and rounded (river stone, shallow, fast water) vs vertical and angular (sheer face).
- Baseline: land/water about 25/75; hardscape = piled river stones with a bank of cobble, a log; plants = Fissidens / Taxiphyllum moss, Hydrocotyle, riparian grass, Anubias on stone; substrate = gravel and cobble; water feature = strong powerhead flow with a riffle; light mood = cool white diffuse "overcast"; open space = small pool downstream.
- Density: open = 3 big boulders / balanced = boulders, cobble, moss / full = rock-and-moss cover.
- Rendering: flowing water surface with foam / normal-map riffles, wet-stone shader, moss decals on rock.
- Water: soft to medium, high oxygen. Temp: 66-74 (cool; pumps add heat, may need a chiller). Min: 30 gal long (40 long preferred).
- Animals: hillstream loaches (Sewellia, Beaufortia), white cloud mountain minnows, Rasbora / danio at the cool end.
- Welfare: flow rates quoted 15-20 turnovers/hour but contested; heat from pumps; ensure outflow areas and rest zones.
- Sources: https://gensou.sg/?p=21257 ; https://modestfish.com/?p=7796 ; https://www.fishforums.net/threads/55g-himalayan-foothills-paludarium.490515

#### cliff-gorge - "Cliff Face Gorge"
- Tagline: "A stone cliff with a thread of water. Mostly rock, mostly mist."
- Cues: tall angular stratified rock rising from a narrow pool; thin seep or trickle; ledges with sparse ferns and moss; bare stone in the foreground.
- Contrast with cascade-wall: stone-dominated and sparse, no bromeliad curtain, montane colors (grey-tan); contrast with rainforest-waterfall: dry ledges and bare rock vs lush.
- Baseline: land/water about 70/30; hardscape = slate / seiryu stacked in strata, one gap forming the gorge; plants = mosses, small ferns, Selaginella, creeping fig in cracks; substrate = rock rubble and sand; water feature = thin trickle or seep, no plunge; light mood = cool grazing sidelight to show rock grain; open space = narrow pool at the foot and sky above.
- Density: open = bare face with two ledges / balanced = ledges, moss, trickle / full = thickly mossed cliff.
- Rendering: stratified rock mesh and normal map, sidelight shadows, thin water decal and slight mist.
- Water: soft to medium. Temp: 66-76. Min: 20 gal tall (18 x 18 x 24 about 34 gal practical).
- Animals: small cool-water fish, shrimp; newts for a cool variant.
- Welfare: unstable stacked rock must be bonded (silicone / foam), ensure no crush hazards for animals.
- Sources: https://bantam.earth/?p=722 ; https://aquifarm.com/paludarium-design-ideas

### FAMILY 5: Fantasy Water Gardens

#### floating-island - "Floating Island"
- Tagline: "Tiny islands that hover on the surface. Roots hang like curtains."
- Cues: a raft of cork or foam planted like an island; root curtain dangling below; reflections on a still surface.
- Contrast with riparium-garden: free-floating raft with roots hanging beneath vs plants anchored in planters on the rim.
- Baseline: land/water about 10/90 (island covers 15-25 % of surface); hardscape = cork raft with moss "hill"; plants = pothos / ferns / Tillandsia / Hydrocotyle on the raft, Salvinia fringes; substrate = fine sand floor; water feature = none; light mood = soft top glow, bright raft; open space = clear water around and below.
- Density: open = one raft / balanced = two rafts with floaters / full = rafts joined with a floater mat.
- Rendering: floating mesh with gentle bob, hanging root curtain strands, mirror-like surface, emersed leaves above rim.
- Water: soft to medium. Temp: 74-80. Min: 20 gal (30 x 12 x 16 in).
- Animals: pencilfish, ember tetras, Boraras rasboras, shrimp, bettas.
- Welfare: raft must leave a clear section of surface for gas exchange and for labyrinth fish; roots can trap small fish; light for lower plants reduced.
- Sources: https://ukaps.org/forum/threads/wabi-kusa-projects.9358/page-2 ; https://fishyhub.com/sg/blogs/blog-detail/wabi-kusa-aquarium

#### riparium-garden - "Rim Garden"
- Tagline: "A leafy rim over a quiet tank. Mind the jumpers."
- Cues: houseplants (pothos, peace lily, ferns) grown from planters on the back / rim with roots in water; foliage above the glass; open-top look.
- Contrast with floating-island: rooted rim planters vs a floating raft; contrast with flooded-forest: houseplant foliage and planters vs woody trunks.
- Baseline: land/water about 10/90; hardscape = back planter strip, small wood; plants = pothos, peace lily, spider plant, ferns, Hygrophila; substrate = sand or soil cap; water feature = none; light mood = daylit shade; open space = clear front water.
- Density: open = 4 planters / balanced = full planter strip with trailing vines / full = foliage wall.
- Rendering: emersed plants above the tank rim (clip-through frame), root curtains in water, planter geometry.
- Water: soft to medium. Temp: 72-80. Min: 20 gal long (29 gal preferred).
- Animals: betta, tetras, guppies, shrimp.
- Welfare: fish jump from open tops (leave 2-3 in freeboard or a screen); pothos and peace lily are toxic to cats / dogs; submerged stems rot, only roots in water; frequent evaporation top-ups.
- Sources: https://gensou.sg/?p=20093 ; https://fishyhub.com/sg/blogs/blog-detail/riparium-aquarium ; https://forum.aquariumcoop.com/topic/22864-peace-lily-roots/

#### wabi-kusa-world - "Wabi-Kusa Bowl"
- Tagline: "A grass ball, a dish of water, a tiny world. Needs trimming."
- Cues: substrate ball planted with grasses and Rotala sitting in a shallow dish; bright emersed greens; small and compact.
- Contrast with floating-island: sits on the bottom in a dish and is small / desktop scale.
- Baseline: land/water about 70/30 (ball above waterline); hardscape = single ball plus pebbles; plants = Rotala, grasses, ferns, moss; substrate = aqua soil ball; water feature = none; light mood = bright white spot; open space = bare dish margin.
- Density: open = one ball / balanced = ball plus pebbles and moss / full = overgrown.
- Rendering: spherical substrate with moss skin and emersed stems, shallow dish water with refraction.
- Water: soft. Temp: 68-78 (room). Min: 1-5 gal bowl (no fish by default).
- Animals: none by default, optionally shrimp in a 5 gal.
- Welfare: no fish in a bowl; light heat and algae; watch evaporation.
- Sources: https://fishyhub.com/sg/blogs/blog-detail/wabi-kusa-aquarium ; https://www.aquasabi.com/aquascaping-wiki/aquascaping/wabi-kusa

### FAMILY 6: Waterside Creature Habitats

#### turtle-shore - "Turtle Basking Shore"
- Tagline: "A basking dock and a deep swim. Needs real water."
- Cues: wide swim-water, a flat basking dock under a lamp, ramp, bare rock or cork shore; turtle silhouette.
- Contrast with newt-shore: warm, bright and open vs cool, shaded and mossy.
- Baseline: land/water about 15/85; hardscape = floating dock or rock slab, smooth driftwood; plants = hardy anubias / java fern, floating plants outside the nibble zone; substrate = bare or large smooth gravel; water feature = canister filter outflow; light mood = bright lamp spot and UVB; open space = deep open swimming lane.
- Density: open = dock only / balanced = dock, wood, a few plants / full = adds plant guard.
- Rendering: basking lamp glow, ramp and dock geometry, reflective water, turtle animation.
- Water: soft to medium. Temp: water 72-78, basking 85-90 (surface up to 95-104 for sliders). Min: 40 gal for ONE adult musk turtle (55 gal preferred); sliders, cooters and painted turtles 75-125+ gal (out of scope for the picker).
- Animals: common musk turtle (Sternotherus odoratus) only; no fish.
- Welfare: UVB tube 5.0-10.0 over the dock, replace every 6-12 months; filter about 2x volume; water depth about 1.5-2x shell length; salmonella hygiene; hatchlings under 4 in cannot be sold in the US; gate this tile below 40 gal.
- Sources: https://www.turtletimes.com/?p=2860 ; https://gensou.sg/musk-turtle-care-guide-aquarium/ ; https://www.zillarules.com/information/care-sheets/musk-turtle

#### newt-shore - "Newt Pond Shore"
- Tagline: "Cool mossy pond with orange-bellied residents. Hates heat."
- Cues: mostly water with a mossy cork island / ramp; dim, green, shaded; orange-bellied newts at the surface.
- Contrast with turtle-shore: cool and shaded with moss and plants vs warm and bright dock.
- Baseline: land/water about 30/70; hardscape = cork bark island, stone ramp; plants = moss, Java fern, Anubias, floating Salvinia, a few ferns on the island; substrate = fine sand; water feature = optional gentle filter; light mood = dim shaded green; open space = open water in front.
- Density: open = one island / balanced = island plus plants and wood / full = mossy thicket.
- Rendering: moss-covered cork mesh, water with gentle ripples, newt sprites, soft shadows.
- Water: soft to medium, dechlorinated (chloramine is acutely toxic). Temp: 60-68, brief 72, avoid above 75-78. Min: 20 gal long (30 x 12 x 12 in) for a pair to four.
- Animals: Chinese / Japanese fire-bellied newts; optional white cloud mountain minnows.
- Welfare: no heater unless the room is very cold; no heat lamp; skin secretions are irritants, wash hands; never house with fire-bellied toads or warm-water fish; secure lid.
- Sources: https://www.petmd.com/exotic/fire-bellied-newt-care-sheet ; https://www.furrycritter.com/pages/products_gear/fish/fire_belly_newt_housing.htm ; https://reptilesmagazine.com/?p=8029

#### crab-shore - "Red-Claw Crab Shore"
- Tagline: "Rock caves and a brackish pool. Escape artists included."
- Cues: piled rocks and cork forming crevices; shallow pool; red-clawed crab on the rocks; no tree canopy.
- Contrast with mudskipper-mudflat: rock crevices and cobble vs a smooth mud flat; contrast with mangrove-paludarium: no trees.
- Baseline: land/water about 50/50; hardscape = rock stack with caves, cork; plants = Anubias, Java fern on rock, a few hardy emersed plants; substrate = coarse sand with shells; water feature = none; light mood = warm moderate light; open space = pool at the front.
- Density: open = one rock stack / balanced = stack plus cork and plants / full = rock maze.
- Rendering: rock cave meshes, wet shore decals, crab agent with claws.
- Water: brackish SG 1.005-1.015. Temp: 75-82. Min: 20-30 gal (24 in wide; 10 gal single-crab minimum quoted).
- Animals: red-claw crab (Perisesarma bidens) alone or small group; no fish tankmates by default.
- Welfare: they crawl out through any gap (lid, sealed tubes); freshwater kills them within weeks in some reports; provide hides; claw risk to fish.
- Sources: https://gensou.sg/?p=20676 ; https://www.aquariumsource.com/red-claw-crab/ ; https://fishkeeper.co.uk/fish/freshwater/brackish-water-fish/red-clawed-crab

#### mudskipper-mudflat - "Mudskipper Mudflat"
- Tagline: "A fish that walks on mud. Needs salt and a lid."
- Cues: broad wet mud-sand beach with burrows; shallow brackish pool; mudskippers perched with raised dorsal fins; few or no trees.
- Contrast with crab-shore: smooth mud and burrows vs rock crevices; contrast with mangrove-paludarium: open flat vs root forest.
- Baseline: land/water about 55/45; hardscape = driftwood roots, a few flat stones; plants = Anubias on wood, hardy emersed grasses; substrate = deep mud-sand slope; water feature = none; light mood = warm lamp glow; open space = wide beach.
- Density: open = bare flat / balanced = flat plus a stump and a few plants / full = adds mangrove sapling (then reclassify as mangrove).
- Rendering: wet-mud shader with burrow pits, shallow brackish water, fish-on-land animation, heat lamp glow.
- Water: brackish SG 1.005-1.015, 2-4 in deep. Temp: 76-84 water/air. Min: 40 gal long (20-30 gal quoted for small groups; 36 x 18 x 16 in preferred).
- Animals: Periophthalmus species (dwarf / Atlantic / African); archerfish or livebearers for tankmates only in larger builds.
- Welfare: land 33-60 % quoted in sources, use at least 40-50 %; high humidity; sealed lid (they jump); territorial, one or a pair; skin must stay wet.
