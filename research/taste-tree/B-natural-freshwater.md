# Taste tree, slice B: natural-look FRESHWATER

Researcher B. Slice: planted aquascaping schools, natural-method / low-tech, biotopes, paludarium / riparium / emersed, natural ponds. Sibling slices: A (artificial / themed / species-driven mainstream), C (marine / reef / brackish). Date 2026-10-30.

Source honesty: web search was thin on primary sources (IAPLC / AGA rule pages did not load as category definitions; most "guides" are retailer SEO blogs, mainly gensou.sg, aquifarm.com). Where a node rests on hobby common knowledge (Amano's books, UKAPS / r/PlantedTank culture, Walstad's book, contest galleries) it is marked (K). Popularity numbers are estimates, labelled as such. The only hard-ish number found: one unsourced market report says ~32 % of hobbyists keep planted tanks (projected 43 % by 2034); ~80/20 freshwater vs saltwater. Treat both as soft.

Units: gallons / inches (litres in parentheses only where useful).

## 0. How the contest world cuts the hobby (framing facts)

- IAPLC (Amano's contest) has NO style categories; it explicitly judges overall appeal, no style or size restrictions, but winners cluster into Nature / Iwagumi / Diorama / Forest / Jungle looks. Source: https://iaplc.com/e/2023/12/08/faq/
- AGA contest: size brackets (<7 gal, 7-30, 30-60, 60-90, >90) x style categories: Aquatic Garden, Dutch, Nature Aquarium, Biotope, Paludarium, Wabi-kusa. Source: https://gensou.sg/aga-aquascaping-contest-guide/
- Biotope Aquarium Design Contest (BAD, biotope-aquarium.info): 2025 categories are regional (North America; Central America and Caribbean; South America; Europe; Africa + Central/Western/Southern Asia; Eastern Asia; Southeast Asia and Oceania), earlier years five continental groups. Habitat entries dominate by Amazon (Rio Negro), Africa (Malawi / Tanganyika) and Southeast Asia (peat swamp, hillstream). Sources: https://www.amazonasmagazine.com/2025/04/25/2025-biotope-aquarium-contest-launches/ , https://biotope-aquarium.info/?p=7312
- AGA / Aquatic Gardeners Association showcase is the best browsable image archive of real contest tanks, tagged "Aquatic Garden", "Biotope Aquascape", "Dutch", "Paludarium": https://showcase.aquatic-gardeners.org/ (examples: Chinese mountain https://showcase.aquatic-gardeners.org/2019/show315.html ; igapo https://showcase.aquatic-gardeners.org/2020/show269.html ; Malaysian peat swamp https://showcase.aquatic-gardeners.org/2013/show537.html)
- Wikipedia lists Nature Aquarium sub-styles as Ryuboku (driftwood), Iwagumi (rock), Diorama. Source: https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aquascape

### Independent VISUAL AXES (useful for the picker; every node below is a point on these)
1. Hardscape amount: none / sparse / dominant (rock-only, wood-only).
2. Plant density and form: carpet+stems / stems only / epiphytes only / none / emersed.
3. Tidy-designed vs wild-messy.
4. Substrate colour: black aquasoil / pale sand / white sand / beige gravel / leaf-covered brown.
5. Water: crystal clear / tea-tinted / murky-green mature.
6. Light colour and strength: bright 6500K white-green vs dim warm amber.
7. Composition: concave (U / bowl), convex (mound / hill), triangular (side-weighted), island / V, flat-and-wide, vertical.
8. Wet vs dry: fully submerged / half-and-half / mostly emersed.

## 2. NODES: aquascape branch

### aquascape — Designed planted aquascape (branch root)
- Aliases/search: aquascaping, planted tank, underwater garden, aquatic garden.
- Parent: natural-freshwater.
- Personas: contest aquascaper, plant geek, "pretty planted tank" fan.
- Visual cues: intentional composition; dense tidy plants, visible hardscape (rock or wood) arranged deliberately, clear water, bright light, usually black or dark substrate, almost no decor ornaments, few and small schooling fish.
- Generic tank: 24-36 in wide "cube / low-iron" 20-40 gal; hardscape centre-left; plants fore-to-back by height.
- Tech: CO2 + ferts + high light (high-tech) typical. Cost med-high. Care high.
- Popularity: core of the contest scene; maybe 15-25 % of freshwater hobbyists post planted layouts, but most on social media (est).
- Distinguisher vs siblings: it looks DESIGNED, like a garden bed, not a fish tank (compare natural-method = looks grown, biotope = looks like a real place).
- Sources: https://www.2hraquarist.com/pages/creating-aquascapes ; https://aquifarm.com/aquascape-styles

### nature-aquarium — Nature Aquarium (Amano style)
- Aliases: Amano style, ADA style, nature style, "NA".
- Parent: aquascape.
- Personas: Amano devotees, contest aquascapers, minimalists, Japanese-garden lovers.
- Visual cues: a recognisable natural LANDSCAPE seen from the front (hills, forest edge, riverbank), asymmetry, depth via small plants in front and taller behind, negative space, black or dark brown aquasoil, crisp lines, bright clear water, one or two plant colour families, small tight schools (neon / rummy-nose / harlequin rasbora).
- Generic tank: 36x18x18 in (~45 gal, 90 cm class); 3-5 stones or 2 wood pieces off-centre; dwarf hairgrass or Glossostigma lawn; stems (Rotala rotundifolia, Ludwigia, Hygrophila) in side and rear; moss on wood. Animals: neon tetra, harlequin rasbora, Amano shrimp, Otocinclus.
- Tech: CO2, ferts, high light. Cost high. Care high.
- Popularity: the dominant "aquascape" image worldwide (est share of scapers ~35 %).
- Distinguisher: one calm landscape with space left open, looks like a Japanese garden under water.
- Sources: https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aquascape ; https://gensou.sg/?p=19379 ; https://iaplc.com/e/

### iwagumi — Iwagumi (rock layout)
- Aliases: iwagumi, "rock formation" style, Sanzon Iwagumi, stone-only layout.
- Parent: nature-aquarium.
- Personas: minimalists, zen fans, contest purists, nano/small-tank owners.
- Visual cues: rocks are the whole design; ONE plant species carpet (Glossostigma, Eleocharis parvula / dwarf hairgrass, Hemianthus callitrichoides, Micranthemum) + at most a second grass; huge negative space; a single schooling species. Looks like a bright green lawn with a stone cluster.
- Generic tank: 24x12x14 in (~16 gal) or 36 in; 1 big Oyaishi, 2 smaller stones; Hemianthus carpet; dwarf hairgrass flanks. Animals: Rummynose or Neon (one species), 2 Otocinclus.
- Tech: CO2 required for carpet, high light; Cost med-high; Care high (carpet maintenance, diatom control).
- Popularity: iconic; fewer actual builds than photos (est few % of planted tanks).
- Distinguisher: only stones + a flat lawn; no wood, no stems.
- Sources: https://www.aquasabi.com/aquascaping-wiki/aquascaping/wabi-kusa (Iwagumi/Amano context) ; https://gensou.sg/?p=25253 ; https://aquifarm.com/aquascaping-types

### iwagumi-sanzon-classic — Classic Sanzon Iwagumi (three-stone)
- Aliases: Sanzon iwagumi, Oyaishi / Fukuishi / Soeishi, 3-stone layout, "triad".
- Parent: iwagumi.
- Personas: traditionalists, bonsai / garden-minded.
- Visual cues: exactly 3 or an odd count of stones (main + secondary + companion, ratio ~ 4:2:1 or 3:2:1), main stone off-centre (golden ratio), carpet in front rising to higher, medium-coloured stone (Seiryu, Ohko, grey/blue-grey).
- Generic tank: 36x18x18; Oyaishi (main, ~12 in) at 1/3 point; Fukuishi 7 in; Soeishi 4 in; Glossostigma carpet; dwarf hairgrass behind.
- Tech: as above; Cost med; Care high.
- Distinguisher: strictly three stones with a clear tall-medium-small order; rest is lawn.
- Sources: https://gensou.sg/?p=25253 ; https://www.aquasabi.com/aquascaping-wiki_aquascaping_wabi-kusa (names of stones) ; (K) Amano, Nature Aquarium World vol.1

### iwagumi-modern-multistone — Modern / "mountain" Iwagumi (many stones)
- Aliases: modern iwagumi, seiryu scape, "stone layout", rockscape, iwagumi with hills.
- Parent: iwagumi.
- Personas: contest scapers, younger social-media crowd.
- Visual cues: 7-20 grey seiryu / ryuoh / frodo-stone pieces forming ridges and valleys, strong grain lines, carpet in valleys, sometimes a third plant (Rotala stems) behind. Reads like miniature mountain range.
- Generic tank: 24-36 in; stacked seiryu ridge diagonally to rear-left; dwarf hairgrass carpet; Rotala sp. 'Green' and Eleocharis; moss bits.
- Tech: high-tech. Cost med-high. Care high.
- Distinguisher: many grey stones form ridges; no strict count, no wood.
- Sources: https://aquifarm.com/aquascape-styles ; https://showcase.aquatic-gardeners.org/2019/show315.html

### ryuboku-wood-nature — Ryuboku (driftwood Nature layout)
- Aliases: ryuboku, driftwood aquascape, wood-centred nature style, "wood scape".
- Parent: nature-aquarium.
- Personas: wood lovers, forest/tree aesthetic, intermediate aquascapers.
- Visual cues: 1-3 knotty driftwood pieces (Mopani, spiderwood, manzanita) dominate; moss, Anubias, Java fern and Bucephalandra on wood; stems and carpet fill gaps; darker, richer, more organic than Iwagumi.
- Generic tank: 36x18x18; main wood left-diagonal; Taxiphyllum moss, Anubias nana petite, Java fern; Rotala behind; Hemianthus in front. Animals: harlequin rasbora, cardinal tetra, Amano shrimp.
- Tech: med to high. Cost med. Care med-high.
- Distinguisher: one big branching wood is the focal element, with moss draped on it.
- Sources: https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aquascape ; https://aquifarm.com/aquascape-styles

### diorama-landscape — Diorama / landscape scape
- Aliases: diorama, landscape aquascape, "scenery scape", forced perspective, mountainscape.
- Parent: nature-aquarium.
- Personas: scene builders, "model-railroad" crowd, contest chasers.
- Visual cues: a SCENE with a story: hill with a trail, cliff, bonsai tree, valley, distant forest; forced perspective (small plants behind, tall in front reversed), height variation, background plants shorter than foreground; hardscape reads as geology.
- Generic tank: 36x24x24 high; stacked rock cliff 2/3 of tank; wood bonsai on top; Hemianthus and moss on slopes; fine sand path.
- Tech: high. Cost high. Care high.
- Popularity: very popular in recent IAPLC top 100 (forum evidence of diorama / NA dominance, https://ukaps.org/forum/threads/iaplc-2024-world-rank-22-closing-a-journal-on-contest-aquascaping.75743/page-2).
- Distinguisher: tall, vertical, theatre-like; reads as a miniature outdoor scene.
- Sources: https://gensou.sg/?p=36818 ; https://aquifarm.com/aquascape-styles

### mountain-cliff-scape — Mountain / cliff scape
- Aliases: mountainscape, cliff scape, canyon scape, "stacked rock".
- Parent: diorama-landscape.
- Personas: contest scapers, rock-collecting artisans.
- Visual cues: tall stacked stone (dragon, seiryu, Mount Tai, Qinglong) in one mass, sheer faces, moss "forest" on the ledges, sometimes narrow waterfall-like Eleocharis streaks, low plant cover.
- Generic tank: 24x24x24 cube; 15-25 rocks glued into a peak at left; moss + Monosolenium on ledges; Hemianthus at base.
- Tech: high. Cost med-high. Care med-high.
- Distinguisher: the rock reaches nearly the water surface as a peak.
- Sources: https://showcase.aquatic-gardeners.org/2016/show417.html ; https://showcase.aquatic-gardeners.org/2009/show99.html

### valley-trail-scape — Valley / trail / path scape
- Aliases: path aquascape, river valley scape, hill-and-trail.
- Parent: diorama-landscape.
- Personas: scenery fans.
- Visual cues: curving pale sand path from foreground to back dividing green hills, small stones as borders, forced perspective, taller plants at rear.
- Generic tank: 36x18x18; two hills of moss / Hemianthus; sand path S-curve; two stones as boulders; one bonsai-like wood.
- Tech: high. Cost med. Care high.
- Distinguisher: the pale winding path.
- Sources: https://aquifarm.com/aquascape-styles ; https://gensou.sg/?p=36818

### bonsai-tree-scape — Bonsai / tree scape
- Aliases: bonsai aquascape, tree on a rock, "tree scape", penjing aquarium.
- Parent: diorama-landscape.
- Personas: bonsai hobbyists, contest scapers.
- Visual cues: a single small branched wood piece (bonsai driftwood) with moss or Microsorum "leaves" crown, mounted on stone; open surroundings; often paired with a moss hill.
- Generic tank: 20x12x12; Sleepy-hollow type bonsai wood centred slightly left; Christmas moss crown; carpet.
- Tech: med-high. Cost med. Care med.
- Distinguisher: single tree focal point with moss crown.
- Sources: https://shop.glassaqua.com/products/bonsai-tree-driftwood-medium-1 ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penjing

### island-lagoon-scape — Island / lagoon scape
- Aliases: island layout, lagoon, "island composition", V-shape / island.
- Parent: diorama-landscape (also a composition option).
- Personas: composition-focused scapers; "islet" lovers.
- Visual cues: central mound of rock + stems + wood surrounded by open water or sand on all sides; swimmers circle it; visible from both sides; often in a bowed / rimless tank.
- Generic tank: 30x30x20 square or 48x24x18 wide; mound at centre; moss and stems; white sand around.
- Tech: high. Cost med-high. Care high.
- Distinguisher: the scape sits like an island with clear water around it.
- Sources: (K) common NA layout taxonomy (Amano concave / convex / island / triangle) ; https://gensou.sg/?p=36818

### forest-scape — Forest scape
- Aliases: forest aquascape, woodland, "tree branch scape", bonsai forest.
- Parent: nature-aquarium (modern).
- Personas: young scapers, forest/anime aesthetic lovers.
- Visual cues: many thin branching spiderwood or manzanita twigs read as TREES; weeping moss or Christmas moss as canopies; fine-leaved stems, floor with brown / leaf-litter or aquasoil; moody mist or tannin tint.
- Generic tank: 24x12x14; 8-12 spiderwood branches vertical; weeping moss; Hygrophila 'Araguaia'; Hemianthus. Animals: ember tetra, chili rasbora, cherry shrimp.
- Tech: med-high. Cost med. Care high (trimming).
- Popularity: frequent contest theme (2025 bronze in a major show, https://aqualibs.com/component/tags/tag/bronze-prize/).
- Distinguisher: vertical fine twigs read as a forest of tiny trees.
- Sources: https://www.cloningaquapets.com/blogs/news/create-your-own-underwater-forest-the-art-of-the-forest-tank-aquascape ; https://gensou.sg/?p=21134

### moss-scape — Moss-heavy scape
- Aliases: mossy scape, moss wall, "mossy mountain", moss hill, Christmas moss.
- Parent: nature-aquarium.
- Personas: moss lovers, shrimp keepers, low-light fans.
- Visual cues: most surfaces coated in moss (Taxiphyllum, Vesicularia, Monosolenium, Fissidens); moss on stone and wood; velvet green mass; few stems.
- Generic tank: 24x12x14; moss-covered rock mound; wood branch with Christmas moss; Fissidens carpet.
- Tech: low-med; Cost low-med; Care med (debris).
- Distinguisher: furry green surfaces, no open lawn.
- Sources: https://gensou.sg/?p=19379 ; (K)

### carpet-lawn-scape — Carpet-only / lawn scape
- Aliases: grass lawn, Hemianthus carpet, minimal lawn scape, "ADA carpet".
- Parent: nature-aquarium.
- Personas: perfectionists, cube-tank owners.
- Visual cues: single flat bright green meadow (HC, Glosso, Monte Carlo, dwarf hairgrass), a few stones or one branch, bubbles from pearling.
- Generic tank: 12x12x12 cube; HC carpet; 3 rocks; one school.
- Tech: high-tech. Cost med. Care high.
- Distinguisher: pearling flat lawn dominates; hardscape token.
- Sources: https://aquifarm.com/aquascaping-types

### nano-pico-scape — Nano / pico scape
- Aliases: nano tank, pico tank, desk scape, cube scape, 2-5 gal.
- Parent: nature-aquarium (any style in miniature).
- Personas: apartment / desk hobbyist, beginners, budget scapers.
- Visual cues: tiny glass (8-16 in), one focal rock or wood, a moss patch, one carpet, 1-2 shrimp / micro-fish; often rimless, with a small LED clip.
- Generic tank: 12x8x8 (3 gal); one rock + HC; shrimp. 
- Tech: low-med; Cost low; Care med-high (water swings).
- Distinguisher: very small glass, a toy-scale world.
- Sources: https://aquifarm.com/aquascape-styles

### dutch — Dutch style
- Aliases: Dutch aquarium, aquatic garden, plant street style, "Dutch scape".
- Parent: aquascape.
- Personas: plant geeks, traditional European societies, colour lovers.
- Visual cues: NO hardscape (or minimal), dense terraced rows of different leaf shapes and colours ("streets"), tall background stems (Rotala, Ludwigia, Cabomba, Hygrophila) pruned in neat bands, bright reds / greens / yellows contrasting, foreground low, 70-80 % plant coverage, clean glass, few fish.
- Generic tank: 48x18x20 (70 gal); terraces of 5-7 species; Ludwigia 'Super Red' mass; Hygrophila corymbosa; Alternanthera; Pogostemon; foreground Cryptocoryne; swimmers: tetras or rasbora.
- Tech: CO2 and ferts, high light. Cost med-high. Care high (trimming weekly).
- Popularity: classic in Europe, formal contests; est <10 % of planted worldwide, strong in Germany, Netherlands.
- Distinguisher: no stones/wood; a coloured flower bed.
- Sources: https://gensou.sg/?p=19379 ; https://theaquariumguide.com/articles/aquascaping-styles-aquariums ; https://aquifarm.com/aquascaping-types

### dutch-streets — Dutch "streets" (terraced)
- Aliases: Dutch terraces, plant streets.
- Parent: dutch.
- Personas: Dutch-society formalists, contest Dutch category.
- Visual cues: distinct bands/terraces of different species at graduated heights, each "street" a single contrasting texture; strict rules (e.g. 'no plant of the same colour adjacent', 'no clumps').
- Tech/cost/care as dutch.
- Distinguisher: strict stair-stepped rows seen from front.
- Sources: https://gensou.sg/?p=19379

### dutch-colour-garden — Dutch colour garden (red/green)
- Aliases: red tank, colour contrast plant tank.
- Parent: dutch.
- Personas: plant-colour fans.
- Visual cues: loose blocks of red / orange / pink (Ludwigia, Rotala 'H'ra', Alternanthera) against green; looks like a flower bed.
- Tech/cost/care as dutch.
- Distinguisher: blazing red and orange stems.
- Sources: (K) ; https://aquifarm.com/aquascaping-types

### jungle-wild — Jungle / wild style
- Aliases: jungle aquascape, overgrown, "jungle tank", wild scape.
- Parent: aquascape (strongly overlaps natural-method).
- Personas: low-maintenance natural fans, beginners, cichlid / angelfish keepers who want plants.
- Visual cues: LOTS of tall plants (Vallisneria, Amazon sword, Sagittaria, Hygrophila, Java fern), no trimming, stems reach the surface and spread, driftwood hidden, floating plants, a deep green-and-brown thicket, fish hide among leaves.
- Generic tank: 48x18x20; 3-5 large Amazon swords; Vallisneria back wall; Java fern on wood; floating Amazon frogbit. Animals: angelfish, tetra, corydoras, pearl gourami.
- Tech: low-tech with ferts or CO2-light. Cost low-med. Care low-med.
- Distinguisher: no negative space; tall leaves hit the surface.
- Sources: https://gensou.sg/?p=36818 ; https://aquifarm.com/aquascape-styles

### taiwanese-terrace — Taiwanese style
- Aliases: Taiwan style, terraced slope, tiered layouts, "stepped slope".
- Parent: aquascape.
- Personas: Asian old-school scapers.
- Visual cues: high terraces of stone and plants rising steeply to the back, lots of depth, many species in compact rows; looks like a terraced hill; mostly historic, now less common.
- Generic tank: 48x24x24; tiered stone steps; mixed red / green stems; sand path.
- Tech: high. Cost med-high. Care high.
- Popularity: niche / historic.
- Distinguisher: steep terraces.
- Sources: https://gensou.sg/?p=36818 (mentions terraces / depth as main characteristic)

### chinese-mountain-stone — Chinese mountain / shanshui stone
- Aliases: Chinese style, mountain scape, shanshui, penjing-inspired, Qinglong stone, dragon stone, Mount Tai stone.
- Parent: aquascape.
- Personas: Chinese contest scapers, penjing-minded artists.
- Visual cues: sculptural, craggy rocks (dragon stone, qinglong) in dramatic vertical stacks suggesting distant mountain ranges, mist and mood, sparse plants, sometimes lit blue-purple; soft ground cover.
- Generic tank: 36x18x24; qinglong-stone peaks; moss and Hemianthus; fine yellowish sand.
- Tech: med-high. Cost high. Care med-high.
- Distinguisher: craggy, stylised mountain-peak rockwork, like a Chinese ink painting.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penjing ; https://showcase.aquatic-gardeners.org/2019/show315.html ; https://showcase.aquatic-gardeners.org/2009/show99.html

### hardscape-only-rock-wood — Hardscape only (rock-and-wood, no plants)
- Aliases: plantless, stone and wood tank, "dry scape in water", rockscape.
- Parent: aquascape.
- Personas: cichlid owners, anti-plant-maintenance people, moss-and-fern-only fans.
- Visual cues: no stems or carpets; stone stacks or wood roots, maybe moss / Anubias on them; sand; clean.
- Generic tank: 48x18x18; stacked slate; two wood roots; sand; cichlids or tetras.
- Tech: none. Cost low-med. Care low.
- Distinguisher: a plantless tank that still looks designed.
- Sources: (K) common in cichlid forums ; https://www.practicalfishkeeping.co.uk/fishkeeping-answers/what-rocks-should-i-use-for-malawi-cichlids/

### plant-collector-showcase — Plant collector tank
- Aliases: rare plant tank, plant geek tank, specimen tank, "one of each".
- Parent: aquascape.
- Personas: plant collector, rare-species hoarders (Crypt, Anubias, Bucephalandra, Aponogeton, Echinodorus).
- Visual cues: many species labelled, patchwork, chaotic mix of leaf shapes, bright; hardscape secondary.
- Generic tank: 36x18x18; 15-20 species in patches.
- Tech: med-high. Cost high. Care high.
- Distinguisher: variety of leaf colours/shapes in small patches; no clear composition.
- Sources: https://scapecrunch.com/threads/should-we-have-strict-aquascaping-styles.176/ (K)

### red-plant-showcase — Red-plant showcase
- Aliases: red scape, Rotala tank, red stem tank.
- Parent: aquascape.
- Personas: colour-hunters, contest competitors in "colour" classes.
- Visual cues: dominant stand of red stems (Rotala 'H'ra', 'Colorata', Ludwigia, Alternanthera) with green foreground and one grey stone; strong white light.
- Generic tank: 24x12x14; Rotala wallichii; Ludwigia; Hemianthus; ember tetra.
- Tech: high. Cost med-high. Care high.
- Distinguisher: blazing red.
- Sources: (K) ; https://aquifarm.com/aquascape-styles

## 3. NODES: natural-method (low-tech) branch

### natural-method — Natural-method / low-tech (branch root)
- Aliases: low-tech planted, no-CO2, nature-run, "set and forget" planted.
- Parent: natural-freshwater.
- Personas: low-maintenance natural fan, beginner, Walstad followers, "real plants but no fuss" crowd.
- Visual cues: slower, softer, greener-and-browner; plants chosen for robustness; less trimming; hardscape often wood; looks "grown in" not "set up"; clear to slightly tinted water.
- Tech: none or light. Cost low. Care low.
- Popularity: large share of everyday planted tanks (est 50-60 % of planted tanks worldwide).
- Distinguisher: soft untidy growth, no bright carpets.
- Sources: https://gensou.sg/walstad-method-low-tech-planted-tank/

### walstad-dirted — Walstad / El Natural dirted tank
- Aliases: Walstad method, dirted tank, soil-and-cap, El Natural, Diana Walstad, Ecology of the Planted Aquarium.

## 8. Persona notes and market facts

- Contest aquascaper: Nature / Iwagumi / Diorama / Forest; high-tech, clean, small tanks; IAPLC scene.
- Plant geek / collector: Dutch, plant-collector-showcase, red-plant-showcase; wants species diversity.
- Low-maintenance natural fan: walstad-dirted, lowtech-epiphyte-tank, riparium, floating-plant canopy.
- Biotope purist: peat swamp, igapo, Malawi rock, hillstream; avoids decor, cares about exact fish / habitat match.
- Blackwater / botanical aesthete: botanical-blackwater, leaf-litter, amazon-blackwater; dark moody look.
- Nano hobbyist: nano-pico-scape, shrimp-moss-nano, wabi-kusa, iwagumi in 8-16 in.
- "Pretty tank, real plants" crowd: living-room-planted-community (biggest real-world cluster).
- Data: no reliable survey of planted vs non-planted. Soft numbers only: ~32 % of hobbyists keep planted tanks (unsourced market report, https://marketintelo.com/report/live-aquarium-plants-market); ~80 % freshwater. Aquarium Friend polled 1,089 aquascapers (Feb 2021), https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/23-9-of-aquascapers-identified-as-lgbtqia-new-aquascaping-study-by-aquarium-friend-finds-301253766.html (demographics only).

## 9. Compositions (orthogonal axis, already in the app)

concave (U / bowl, high at both sides, open centre: Nature classic), convex (central mound / hill), triangular (high one side, sloping to the other: Iwagumi, Nature), island / V (mound in the middle, water on all sides), flat wide meadow (carpet / Dutch rows). Dutch ignores these (it uses terraced rows).
