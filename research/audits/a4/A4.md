# A4: performance pass on the heavy arthropod models (2026-10-28)

Measured with `node scripts/a4-perf.mjs` (terrarium example, hardware GPU, 1 and 6 animals; GPU ms = paired A/B on alternating frames, animals' materials hidden / shown, 40 pairs; raw numbers in before.json / after.json). The triangles and draw calls are the animals' own visible meshes.

| model | tris before | tris after | draw calls before | after | GPU ms added by 6, before | after |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| curly hair tarantula | 246,430 | 57,244 at tank distance (LOD far coat); 233,022 close up | 12 | 5 | +13.2 | +1.7 |
| emperor scorpion | 136,504 | 56,472 | 22 | 5 | +2.9 | +1.0 |
| Indian stick insect | 66,880 | 40,664 | 16 | 4 | +0.3 | +0.3 |
| orchid mantis | 45,232 | 31,488 | 14 | 4 | +0.8 | +0.5 |

(6 animals: curly hair 1,478,580 -> 343,464 tris, 72 -> 30 draw calls; scorpion 819,024 -> 338,832, 132 -> 30; stick 401,280 -> 243,984, 96 -> 24; mantis 271,392 -> 188,928, 84 -> 24. GPU deltas under 0.5 ms are inside the machine's noise.)

## What dominated
- Draw calls: every limb was two meshes (near and far side), the solids three, the eyes two to four. All baked into one mesh per material per model (limbs, solids, eyes, eyelid mounds), body stays its own mesh: 4-5 calls.
- Scorpion / stick: the warted body grid (200 x 120 = 48k triangles) and the 560-triangle sphere under every hair, keel and spine solid (about 60 of them, each mirrored).
- Tarantula: hair strands (8 triangles per curly strand, about 230k of the 246k).

## What changed (model files only)
- render/herp3d.ts (arthropods only, gated on `art in ARTH3D`; frogs / lizards / crabs unchanged): merged meshes (`bake`, `mergeByMaterial`, cached per model), warted body grid 150 x 96 (was 200 x 120), solid facets by size (hairs and keels 8 x 5 or 12 x 8, big solids keep 20 x 14), leaner leg tubes (`lean`).
- render/limbs.ts: `fuzz` option `segs` (curl pieces, default 4 = unchanged), `bandedChain(..., lean)`.
- render/tarantula3d.ts: the eight eyes in one mesh; the coat as an LOD (`CoatLOD`): the full coat (unchanged strands) when the body length is 10 % or more of the view's height, a far coat (20 % of the strands, 3x wider, curls in two pieces) below that. In the default terrarium view a 57 mm tarantula is 3 % of the view height, so it draws the far coat; zoomed in close (and on every model sheet) the full coat. Lean leg tubes (10 radial instead of 12) in both.
- Tests: tarantula.test.ts reads the eyes from the eye mesh's userData and expects the two-level coat.
- Re-recorded with `--record --only=`: africanmillipede, chacogoldenknee (+ ~f), curlyhair, emperorscorpion, hissingroach, indianstick, orchidmantis, pinktoe, redknee (the tarantulas and the arthropod family share the changed code).

## Not done
- Close up (camera within about 10 body lengths) the tarantula still draws its full 233k-triangle coat: that is the quality the sheets show and was not cut. The other tarantulas (pulchripes, goldenknee, redknee, pinktoe: straight hair, 26,000 + 6,500 per leg strands in art/tarantulas.ts, about 140k triangles) get the same LOD at 20 % of the strands but were not measured in the tank.
- The jumping spider (spider3d.ts) and crabs are other models and untouched.
