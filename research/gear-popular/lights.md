## What the app needs to draw these

1. Slim extendable-bracket bar (15 of 19): black aluminium slab, 60-100 mm wide, 10-20 mm thick, body length near the tank's minimum fit, wire-loop brackets at each end that slide out to the tank length. LEDs face down: 2-5 rows of white dots with sparse coloured RGB dots; marine (NICREW ClassicLED) alternates white and blue; NICREW C10 is a single column of ring clusters. The controller is an inline cord box (or LCD box / remote), not on the bar.
2. Rim-leg slab (hygger Clip On, SEAOURA clip): flat black cover on two folding legs that grip the rim.
3. Gooseneck clip nano (DaToo): 70 mm round disc head on a bendable neck with a screw clamp.
4. Submersible bar (hygger 24/7): small black stick, 200-400 mm, suction cups, sits in the water.

Body is black on every one; the variation is length, LED rows and controller. Lens is clear on all.
