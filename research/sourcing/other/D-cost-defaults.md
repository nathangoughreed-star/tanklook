Reading the result: the physical-gear bands (heater, filter, lids, CO2, skimmer) mostly hold, with the filter base too high and the heater base a little low. The livestock/plant bands that fail are mostly too LOW at the bottom (easy and medium plants, wood), because retail listings rarely go under $5. Tank classes are noisy (street prices vary by brand and sales); n >= 10 per class so the suggestions are usable. Stand fit has 2 points and non-monotone ranges: do not apply without more stand listings.

## Bands the listings cannot cover (keep as labelled assumptions)

- gear.lightUsdPerW: no light catalogue yet (research has 18 Amazon lights, wattage and price not in a data file); can be fitted once lights have watts.
- gear.substrateUsdPerL: rows are per bag (lb), no litre volume cited; needs bag-volume conversion.
- gear.filter.upkeepUsdMo, gear.co2.upkeepUsdMo, gear.skimmer.upkeepUsdMo: need replacement-media / refill cadence, not prices; one-off judgement.
- animals.*.upkeep (food USD/mo): no food-cost-per-animal listings; depends on feeding rate.
- animals.*.mins, plants.minPerWeekEach, gear.co2/ferts.minPerWeek: care minutes are not retail data.
- time.* (water-change minutes, testing, filter cleaning, yearly jobs, mist, salt extra): no source; would need keeper surveys or timed trials.
- gear.heater.wattsPerGal and duty, gear.filter.watts, lightHoursPerDay: engineering rules (watts per gal is checkable from heater galMax vs watts in the catalogue; duty is climate dependent).
- time.waterChangeFraction.*: husbandry convention, not a price.
- risk.* (careLevel, plantDifficulty, tankSmall, salt, animalWarnPts, squashScale, cut): modelled points, no listing can supply them.
