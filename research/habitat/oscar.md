What goes wrong:
- Weakest of three takes the beatings ("most beatings", MFK 3-oscar thread). Rec there: single, pair, or group of 6 "to have enough bodies".
- One dominant, submissive fish cut fins / hides in corner, may die; aggression starts at maturity 2-3 yr; only fix was permanent separation (MFK 7862015).
- Pair bonding is the stated success condition (MFK 8531729); strangers adults often fight; juveniles raised together do better (search summaries).
- Buy group, rehome extras when two pair off (MFK 8531729).

Disagreement on pair size: 75 gal (petfishonline, summary) vs 125 (furrycritter) vs ~150 (MFK pair of 12 in) vs 125-300 (MFK M/F pairs). Per extra oscar: +20-30 (retail summaries) vs +50 (furrycritter) vs 10 gal per adult inch (duanes, ~115-140 gal per 12-14 in oscar, no fixed first-fish base).
Trio: 150 (one owner) vs 180-230 vs 300+. Spread is 2x.

## 2. Oscar with other fish
- Compatible per sources: large loricariid/doradid catfish, characins, anostomids, other cichlids (seriouslyfish REF); silver dollars as "dithers", large pleco, roseline sharks, blood parrots, severum (MFK https://monsterfishkeepers.com/forums/threads/oscar-tankmates.764572/page-2 and goto/post?id=8531729, HOBBY).
- Seriouslyfish: not with much smaller fish; community only in "all but the largest private aquaria".
- Does tankmate raise the size? Yes, per MFK tankmates thread: "Before you get more fish, you need more gallons"; large fish add waste. No number given. furrycritter: companions must be large, size on adult dimensions, no number.
- Only numbered scheme found: fishtankadvisor https://fishtankadvisor.com/oscar-tank-mates/?amp=1 (VEND/blog): per-tankmate "minimum tank" column; base 75 gal for the oscar. Examples: clown loach x5 150 (+75), pleco 100 (+25), giant gourami 200, silver arowana 250, severum 75 (+0), silver dollar x5 75 (+0), bichir 90. The "extra" is max(tankmate min, 75) - 75, i.e. NOT additive: a school of 5 silver dollars adds 0 gal (est from its table).
- Crowding vs compatibility: sources treat tankmates as compatibility (eaten / harassed / nipped) plus a bioload note; none gives a per-fish load rule. One MFK comment: tankmates added as "distraction" to deflect aggression off the lowest-ranked oscar (3-oscar thread).

## 3. Why the size: waste vs room vs aggression
- Waste: furrycritter (heavy bioload; overcrowding degrades water); seriouslyfish (two filters 1080-1350 lph each, 4-5 turnovers/hr, 30-50% weekly change); MFK: one oscar "fouls" a 225 gal in about a week (anecdote); duanes 40-50% changes near daily in smaller tank. Waste can be managed by maintenance (6x2x2 for three "with heavy maintenance").
- Swimming room: furrycritter wants length and width, not height; MFK 125 -> 180 improved one oscar's behaviour (anecdote); seriouslyfish gives a footprint (150 x 60), not a volume.
- Aggression distance: furrycritter "chronic stress, increased aggression" in undersized tanks; duanes, MFK: trouble "usually caused by too small a tank". Sight-line breaks recommended.
- No source separates the three with numbers.

## Summary
1. Only seriouslyfish (REF) is authoritative: 150 x 60 cm base for one adult, "best in a pair"; no group count or per-extra-fish figure.
2. Extra-oscar rules are vendor/hobby and disagree: +20-30 gal (retail), +50 (furrycritter), 10 gal per adult inch (one MFK keeper, ~115 gal per 12-in oscar).
3. Pair 75-150 gal, trio 150-300+ gal; keepers agree only bonded M/F pairs are reliable, males together fail, third fish takes beatings.
4. Tankmates: widely recommended (large catfish, silver dollars, severum, pleco); "more gallons" is said but never quantified except fishtankadvisor's non-additive table.
5. Nothing sourced on tankmate load per fish, or on how a tankmate changes an oscar-group tank size.
6. Size reasons are cited qualitatively (waste, footprint, aggression); no source apportions them, and Aquarium Co-Op / PFK / OATA were not reached.

### 2. Oscar with other fish
- Hepper: tankmates only for "passive" large fish; lists severum, Jack Dempsey, jaguar, arowana, convict, silver dollar. No tank-size change stated; "best to keep your aquarium an Oscar only tank". Angelfish "not a good idea".
- Co-Op: silver dollars, certain plecos, medium South American cichlids worked; nothing about extra gallons.
- FKW: bichirs, firemouth, silver dollars, jaguar, green terror, sailfin pleco, convict, arowana, severum; "make sure there's enough room"; no number.
- PFK (Bleher): only largish tankmates, "similar in size to the Astronotus"; large loricariids and doradids; no number.
- Net: none of the four gives gallons added per tankmate. fishtankadvisor's non-additive table (first pass) remains the only numbered scheme. Hepper's own one-oscar-per-+30 is the only per-fish figure, and it counts oscars, not tankmates.

### 3. Why the size: waste vs room vs aggression
- Waste: Hepper filter >=3x tank volume per hour, "messy"; FKW 30-50% weekly changes; PFK Bleher 20-30% weekly; PFK Hill: overgrown fish "producing so much waste" filtration cannot cope (goldfish example, general).
- Swimming room: Co-Op 75 gal chosen "so that they have more swimming space to turn around"; PFK sizes by fish length x activity (6x length, width 2x), not volume; Hemdal ratio uses length+width, deliberately drops height.
- Aggression: Co-Op trio bullying is a pairing effect (two paired, third bullied), not distance; FKW "tensions can rise in a tank with limited space"; Hepper oscars "territorial".
- PFK is the only source that explains a sizing basis: length and width scale with fish length and activity; volume and waste are not in its formula (REF). Vendors give gallon figures without a stated basis.
