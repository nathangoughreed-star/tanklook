## Findings (2026-10-21)
_The percentages below were measured before lane HB (floor limit, adult sizes). The table above is on the current engine; before / after S196 on that base is listed in HANDOFF.md, section S196._
Run 1, the engine as it stood: median 68 %, 12 lists over 100 %, 8 of them nano schools in 5-20 gal (101-145 %).

**Engine change (Nathan: trust the experts over AqAdvisor; space has diminishing returns per fish).** The per-species factors had been fitted at one count each (mostly 20 fish in 55 gal) and then applied linearly. Now:
- Space per species = SCHOOL_REF x (n / SCHOOL_REF)^0.85 fish (SCHOOL_REF 10). Filtration (waste) stays linear.
- Animals under 2 g count 0.8x their AqAdvisor-fitted space (TINY_EXPERT).
- How the setting was chosen: a grid of exponent 1 / 0.85 / 0.7 / 0.6 / 0.5 against tiny 1 / 0.8 / 0.65. This was the only setting that lowered the scatter of both sets: published lists sdlog 0.40 -> 0.37, AqAdvisor 24 0.34 -> 0.23 (median ours / AqAdvisor 0.96 -> 1.05).
- Rejected: AqAdvisor-free class factors for nano fish (scatter 0.47; neon, cardinal and danio rows sit below the class median).

**After (the table above):** median 71 %, 10 over 100 %.
- Still over: nano sets at 101-126 % (Co-Op 10 gal betta and gourami sets, 15 danios in 20 gal, Fishkeeping World 10 gal tetras), 10 angelfish in 55 gal 156 %, Aqueon's 4 fancy goldfish in 29 gal 186 %, Gensou combo A 114 % (the article itself calls it over budget), and the flowerhorn (filtration 111 %).
- The scatter of about +-40 % among the published lists is their own: it is flat across tank size (10 gal lists read 46-139 %), and many lists are set by temperament (ADFs, pea puffers, an apisto pair), not room.
- The lists the sources call "full" read 46-88 %, so nano lists are denser than the same authors' own full lists in bigger tanks.

**Caveats.**
- Low readings that are not evidence: Co-Op 55 gal tiger barbs and 40 gal saulosi are parts of larger plans.
- Quotes come from page-fetch summaries; spot-check them.
- The 10 gal Co-Op sets were composed from "pick one" menus.
- There are no 75 or 125 gal lists.
