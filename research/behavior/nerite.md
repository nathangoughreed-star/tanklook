# Nerite snail (Neritina / Vittina natalensis): ethogram (R208)

Source: a ChatGPT answer to the scoped prompt, gathered by Nathan on 2026-11-28. This is a digest; the full answer is in the C25 chat transcript.

**Character:** "a surface-bound, persistent grazer with long, irregular activity and resting cycles." It moves according to **where the algae and biofilm are**, not toward arbitrary destinations: it works one patch, slowly uses it up and **leaves visible clean trails**. Hardly any social awareness. Long periods without moving are normal.

**Movement:** glides on muscular waves along a broad flat foot, the shell carried smoothly with little rocking. Turns by steering the head and the front of the foot, the rest following. Climbs vertical surfaces and crawls upside down the same way. **Corner transition**: reaches the front of the foot onto the next surface, then shifts its body across. Disturbed: pulls in its antennae, head and foot, and closes the operculum if threatened. **Flipped over, it reaches for a nearby surface to lever itself up; it can stay stuck on open flat substrate.**

**Ethogram:** glides on any surface (very often); grazes glass in winding paths with clean tracks (very often); grazes rock and wood following the contours (very often, often); works back and forth over one patch (often); climbs up and down (very often); crawls upside down under wood or rock (sometimes); crosses substrate (sometimes); grazes sturdy leaves without harming them (sometimes); feels around with its antennae (often); eats prepared food (some individuals). Rests stuck to a surface (very often); stays still for very long, sometimes closed up (sometimes); rests in shelter (sometimes); grazes at night (often); **explores along the waterline, rests above the water, may climb out of the tank** (normal, not a water-quality sign). Passes other snails, grazes near them, climbs over shells; shrimp pick at its shell and it doesn't care; ignores fish; mating contact; females leave white egg capsules on hard surfaces (they don't hatch in freshwater). Pulls in when touched, closes up when harassed, falls off, tries to right itself, gets stuck upside down; stress = staying closed or not attaching.

**Generic-animation mistakes (its list):** always moving; random paths ignoring the algae; sliding without the foot and shell following the surface; the shell rolling like a wheel; long waving antennae; treating the waterline as a wall; always flipping upright after a fall; hiding every time a fish passes; snails following each other; mystery-snail behaviors (siphon, floating).
