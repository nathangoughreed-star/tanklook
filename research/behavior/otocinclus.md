# Otocinclus (Otocinclus vittatus / affinis): ethogram (R208)

Source: a ChatGPT answer to the scoped prompt, gathered by Nathan on 2026-11-28. This is a digest; the full answer is in the C25 chat transcript.

**Character:** its life is surfaces, not water volume: a network of leaves, stems, glass, wood and rock used for feeding, resting and as destinations. Swimming is mostly the move between surfaces. Social life is a loose pull toward companions and shared grazing spots, not schooling. **The loop: attach, graze, reposition, rest, let go, dart, reattach.** It can look motionless for a long time, then suddenly be the fastest fish in the tank.

**Movement:** lets go of its sucker mouth, flexes and pushes off with the tail, accelerates fast with short side-to-side beats, brakes and reattaches. Pectorals steady and position the body against the surface. While attached it moves by small mouth repositions, fin adjustments and wiggles. Pivots around its mouth, crawls round curved surfaces, any angle including fully upside down. At rest the tail and body are still apart from breathing. **Moving between surfaces is much faster than moving across them.**
