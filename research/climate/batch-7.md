# Climate batch 7: 8 freshwater fish (water temperature only)

Compiled 2026-10-19. O = page opened and read; S = search snippet only. C = captive recommendation, W = wild. FishBase and Wet Web Media not reachable (cert error).
No source gave wild water temperature for any species except Lake Malawi (Wikipedia, opened: surface 24-29 C, deep about 22 C) and the Llanos for the German blue ram.

## 1. Pearl gourami (Trichopodus leerii)
- Seriously Fish (O, C): 24-30. https://www.seriouslyfish.com/species/trichopodus-leerii/
- tankarium (O, C): 25-28 (77-82F). https://www.tankarium.com/pearl-gourami/
- Aquarium Source (O, C): 25-28. https://www.aquariumsource.com/pearl-gourami/
- Wikipedia (O): no temperature.
- Search roundup of care guides (S, C): mostly 25-28; some 22-28 or 24-30.
**Synthesis:** comfortable 25-28, tolerable 22-30. Agreement good. Room air should be near water temperature.

## 2. German blue ram (Mikrogeophagus ramirezi)
- Seriously Fish (O, C): 27-30, "needs 27 or warmer". https://www.seriouslyfish.com/species/mikrogeophagus-ramirezi/
- Aquarium Co-Op (O, C): 29-30. https://www.aquariumcoop.com/blogs/aquarium/german-blue-ram
- Wikipedia (O, W): 25.5-29.5 (secondary, untraced). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikrogeophagus_ramirezi
- Fishkeeper (O, C): 25-30. https://www.fishkeeper.co.uk/fish/freshwater/cichlids/electric-blue-ram
- biotope-aquarium.info (S, W): noon readings in Rio Apure 27.8-29.2; dry-season/January 22-26 mentioned.
**Synthesis:** comfortable 27-30, tolerable 24-32. Edge values are judgement. Aquarium Co-Op is warmest; Fishkeeper lower.
