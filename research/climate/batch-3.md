# Climate batch 3: temperature (and humidity) for 8 species

Compiled 2026-10-19. F converted to C (rounded). "Opened" = page fetched and read; "snippet" = figure from a search-result summary only (page not opened, fetch failed, or no number on page). Type: W = wild, C = captive-care.
In batch-3.json, for the two species with both water and air (firetoad, firenewt) `ok`/`tol` are WATER and air goes in extra fields `airOk`/`airTol`. sourcesOpened counts only opened pages that gave a usable figure. FishBase loaded for the mandarin but gave only qualitative climate; the Plymouth thesis host was unreachable.
