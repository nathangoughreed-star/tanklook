# Climate batch 5: 8 small freshwater fish (water temperature only)

Compiled 2026-10-19. F converted to C. O = page opened and read, S = search snippet only. W = wild, C = captive recommendation. Seriously Fish (SF) pages opened for all 8; none give a measured wild temperature except zebra danio (July only) and cherry barb (air only). FishBase: 403 on CPD fetch, skipped. Co-Op pages 404 for harlequin and hatchetfish.

## 1. Harlequin rasbora (Trigonostigma heteromorpha)
- SF (O, C): 21-28. https://www.seriouslyfish.com/species/trigonostigma-heteromorpha/
- FishProfiles (O, C): 22-25. https://fishprofiles.com/profiles/freshwater/Cyprinids/Trigonostigma_heteromorpha
- Fish Tank Advisor (O, C): 23-28, breeding 28. https://fishtankadvisor.com/harlequin-rasbora/
- Hepper (S, C): 22-25. https://www.hepper.com/Harlequin-rasbora/
- Tankflare (S, C): 22-27. https://www.tankflare.com/blogs/harlequin-rasbora-care-guide
- Wild: none found.
Synthesis: comfortable 22-26; tolerable 20-28. Disagreement: conservative 22-25 vs wide 21/23-28.

## 2. Chili rasbora (Boraras brigittae)
- SF (O, C): 20-28. https://www.seriouslyfish.com/species/boraras-brigittae/
- Aquarium Co-Op (O, C): 22-28. https://www.aquariumcoop.com/blogs/aquarium/chili-rasbora
- Practical Fishkeeping (O, C): 20-28. https://www.practicalfishkeeping.co.uk/features/chilli-rasbora-care-guide-boraras-brigittae/
- Tankarium / modestfish (S, C): 20-28, ideal about 23 (74F). https://www.tankarium.com/chili-rasbora/
- Wild: blackwater peat swamp, qualitative only; no temperature.
Synthesis: comfortable 23-27; tolerable 20-29. Sources agree closely; Borneo peat swamp makes the warm side plausible.

## 3. Zebra danio (Danio rerio)
- SF (O, C+W): 18-25; wild July 24.6-38.6. https://www.seriouslyfish.com/species/danio-rerio/
- Aquarium Co-Op (O, C): 18-24. https://www.aquariumcoop.com/blogs/aquarium/zebra-danio
- Wikipedia (O, W): field surveys mostly 16.5-34, outliers 12.3 and 38.6. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zebrafish
- Biobide (O, lab C): 24-28, peak 28; CTmin 6.2 / CTmax 39.2 acclimated to 20 C. https://biobide.com/temperature-needs-danio-rerio
- Lab guidance summary (S, C): 26-28.5, tolerated 24-29. https://umcms.um.edu.my/sites/resfom-um-edu-my/img/files/4_%20Guidance%20on%20Housing%20and%20Care%20of%20Zebrafish_2011.pdf
Synthesis: comfortable 20-26; tolerable 15-32 (wild 6-38 survives, not for display). Disagreement: hobby 18-25 vs lab 26-28.5; both inside the wild range.

## 4. White cloud mountain minnow (Tanichthys albonubes)
- SF (O, C): 14-22, "most comfortable". https://www.seriouslyfish.com/species/tanichthys-albonubes/
- Aquarium Co-Op (O, C): 18-25, no heater needed. https://www.aquariumcoop.com/blogs/aquarium/white-cloud-mountain-minnow
- Wikipedia (O, W nominal): 18-22; survives down to 5. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Cloud_Mountain_minnow
- fishtanksetups (O, C): 15.5-22 (60-72F), stress above 24 (75F). https://fishtanksetups.com/white-cloud-mountain-minnow-care/
- FishBase (S, W): 18-22, to 5. https://www.fishbase.se/summary/4758
Synthesis: comfortable 16-22; tolerable 10-26. Disagreement: Co-Op upper 25; wild/hobby cluster 18-22. Wild figure is secondhand (species nearly gone from the wild).
