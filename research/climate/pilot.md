# Climate pilot: temperature (and humidity) for 6 species

Compiled 2026-10-19. F converted to C (rounded). "Direct" = page fetched and read; "via search" = figure came from a search-result summary only (page not opened or fetch failed). "Secondary" = a page quoting another source (Merck, LafeberVet, Schaffer 1989).
Type: W = wild habitat, C = captive-care recommendation.
