# Climate batch 2: water temperature for 8 marine fish

Compiled 2026-10-19. F converted to C (rounded). "Opened" = page fetched, figure read from the page. "Snippet" = search-result summary only. Type: W = wild habitat, C = captive-care recommendation. All marine: water temperature only (no humidity). Seriously Fish has no marine catalogue (its marine pages return "not in the catalogue"); Aquarium Co-Op and FishBase were not used (FishProfiles record for firefish missing). Most marine care sources are retailers; independent or academic temperature data is thin.
