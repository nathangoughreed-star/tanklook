## Found but not modelled

- **Seachem Laterite**: an additive, not a bed; no US price page found.
- **Tropica Aquarium Soil**: one listing (J&L Aquatics, $26.96 for 3 L) whose country and currency we could not confirm; no grain photo.
- **Walstad potting soil**: Home Depot and Ace refused reads; no $/lb.
- **Netlea / Aquarium Co-op / Mr Aqua soils**: no US listing found, or the site rate-limited us.
- **Fluval Biomax**: filter media, not a substrate.
- **Estes (Spectrastone, Special, Shallow Creek, Permaglo)**: real products, but their colours and grain sizes were not read from any page (estimated only), so they are not drawn. There is no 'Spectrum Brands Estes' line: Spectrum owns GloFish, Tetra and Marineland; Estes is a separate maker.
- **Black Diamond blasting grit** at Tractor Supply is coal slag, an industrial abrasive not sold for aquariums.
- **Other colours** each maker sells (Top Fin has 13, GloFish 5, Imagitarium 7+): only the ones with sampled photo colours or a clear name are in the picker.

## Clown puke

Every big chain sells a multicolour mix: Top Fin Rainbow Mix (pink, orange, yellow, green, blue, sampled from the bag photo), Top Fin Mardi Gras (pink, purple, green, blue, teal), GloFish pink/green/blue, Imagitarium Rainbow Confetti, Estes Permaglo Rainbow. The app's **Clown puke** preset is a custom blend of five dyed gravels (red, blue, yellow, green, pink, 20 % each, fully mixed) so it can be reshuffled and edited; the Top Fin and GloFish mixes are also pickable as products.

- **Fluval Plant & Shrimp Stratum**: Volcanic soil granules from Japan; the maker says they soften water and lower carbonate hardness without staining.
- **CaribSea Eco-Complete Black**: Basaltic volcanic gravel with iron and other minerals, packed wet with live bacteria; the maker says it will not raise pH or hardness. Sizes run from fine to chunky.
- **CaribSea Eco-Complete Red**: Shale with no dyes or coatings. The pile mixes rust red, brown and near-black chips, so the bed reads dark brick red.
- **Seachem Flourite Red**: Seachem does not state a grain size; 3-6 mm is read off the photo.
- **Seachem Flourite Dark**: Seachem does not state a grain size; 3-6 mm is read off the photo.
- **ADA Aqua Soil Amazonia**: Japanese soil with a nutrient supplement; makers and retailers say it lowers hardness and brings pH to about 5.5-6.5. Sold by the litre.
- **UNS Controsoil, Black**: Retailer says it holds pH near 6.8 and lowers hardness. Sold by the litre; Fine Black shown.
- **CaribSea Arag-Alive Special Grade**: Live aragonite reef sand, pre-packed with bacteria; holds pH near 8.2. Do not rinse; pour in gently.
- **CaribSea Arag-Alive Natural Reef**: Live aragonite reef sand in natural reef colours; holds pH near 8.2. Do not rinse.
- **CaribSea Arag-Alive Fiji Pink**: Live aragonite reef sand, white with a pink cast; holds pH near 8.2. Do not rinse.
- **CaribSea Florida Crushed Coral**: Coarse crushed coral that buffers pH for saltwater, reef or cichlid tanks; rinse until the water runs clear.
