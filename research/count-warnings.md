# Count warnings: re-evaluation (2026-10-22, lane CW; Nathan: "we need to reevaluate every one of those warnings")

Classes: **a** fighting / territory (keep, sourced) · **b** room or waste (remove: the Stocking % covers it) · **c** minimum group (keep as advice) · **d** no basis (remove, or source it).
Picture: count-warnings-screen.png.

## Where the app warns about how MANY

| place | wording | fires for | proposal |
|---|---|---|---|
| ui/stockPlanLogic.ts `overWarn` | "More than N X in one tank is not advised: expect fighting or crowding" / "X is best kept one to a tank: expect fighting" / "More than about N X in a tank this size may fight" | every species past its flat `max` (74) | only species with a fighting limit (same rule as the Fullness tile) |
| ui/stockPlanLogic.ts `notesOf` | "Group size: Great (at the most one tank takes: N)" | n = max, max > 1 | only for fighters |
| scene/territory.ts `crowdWarnings` (Fullness tile, sidebar) | "N X: more than about M in a tank this size may fight" | fighters only (S196) | unchanged |
| scene/starter.ts `fightMax` | (starter cap, no text) | fighters only | unchanged |
| scene/animalfit.ts | "keeps one per tank, you already have one" | `solo` species (13) | keep (a) |
| scene/herpcheck.ts | "N animals: sources support at most M together. ..." | herpstock rules (11 herps) | keep 7 (a); drop max for White's tree frog, fire-bellied toad, newt, ADF (b) |
| data/groups.ts band text | "Too few: wants at least N" and per-species avoid lines | n < bands.ok | keep (c) |
| data/groups.ts platy `great` text | "Groups of 7+ may fight; keep sexes balanced" | platy n >= 6 | (d) reword to "Great; keep more females than males" |
| data/grazers.ts `grazerShort` | "feed X, or keep N" | grazers past the algae supply | keep: food, sourced R5 (not room, not fighting) |
| scene/plan.ts | "too many bottom dwellers for its floor" | floor > 100 % | keep: this IS the room measure |

Rule after the change: **a species keeps a `max` only if it is a fighting limit** (one to a tank, one pair, or a sourced territory rule). Male-on-male fighting in harem species stays where it already is: the "Kept 1 male to N females ... Your mix is short on females" line.
