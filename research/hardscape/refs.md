## Spider wood (`spider`)
- 1.jpg: yourlocalfishstore.com/products/w-y-s-i-w-y-g-104-large-spiderwood ; img cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0837/5104/3390/files/library-BE7B115B-E570-4E7D-BD25-C95D29E5EC52-L0-001.jpg
- 2.jpg: yourlocalfishstore.com/products/w-y-s-i-w-y-g-608-large-piece-spiderwood ; img cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0837/5104/3390/files/IMG_9307.jpg
- Only 2 photos; no in-tank shot.
Look:
- Shape: one thick, short root stub or flat-cut block at the base, from which 3-6 thin limbs fan up and out; limbs fork 1-3 times into thin twigs and end in a point or a snapped tip. Asymmetric: one limb often long and near-horizontal, the rest curving up. Open, airy silhouette (many gaps), 2-3x wider/taller than the base is thick.
- Thickness: limbs taper from about 0.5-1.5 in at the root to under 0.25 in at the tips; length-to-thickness 15-40:1. Gentle S-curves, no sharp kinks.
- Texture: bare, no bark; smooth sun-bleached grain with fine longitudinal cracks and small knots; base rougher and knobbed.
- Colour: pale tan `#c9a37a`, light wood `#d9b98d`, grey-brown `#8a6d4f` in creases; tips and fresh breaks lighter `#e6cfa8`.
- Size: 6-24 in, most 8-16 in.

## Malaysian driftwood (`malaysian`)
- 1.jpg: tropicflow.com/products/malaysian-driftwood ; img cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0667/6237/5466/files/malaysian-driftwood-1.jpg (in-tank: bare cube, stacked dark pieces with stones)
- 2.jpg: same page ; img cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0667/6237/5466/files/malaysian-driftwood-aquascape-1.jpg (in-tank, planted; wood looks red-brown under warm light)
- Both are tank shots; no clean single-piece product photo found.
Look:
- Shape: chunky, gnarled root-stump mass; short thick branches (length about 1-3x thickness) stubbing upward from a heavy base, plus lumpy knuckles. Irregular, twisted, with hollows and holes through the wood. Reads as fused roots, not a branching tree.
- Texture: very rough, deeply ridged and grooved, bark-free; pitted, rotted pockets, rough broken ends; looks dense and heavy.
- Colour: near black `#2a2420` to dark brown `#3b2f27`; worn high edges grey-brown `#6b5a4a`; reddish-brown in warm light `#7a4a30`.
- Size: 6-24 in, typically 8-18 in.

## Mopani wood (`mopani`)

Look:

## Manzanita (`manzanita`)
- 1.jpg: aquascaperoom.ca/manzanita-driftwood/ ; img cdn11.bigcommerce.com/s-zdr7z7ycdt/products/4309/images/26835/manz2__60971.1777568828.500.750.jpg (500 px, watermarked)

- 2 photos, both under 700 px.
Look:
- Shape: two kinds. (a) Driftwood piece: a thick smooth curving trunk (4-6x longer than wide) splitting into 2-3 limbs with Y-forks, twisted. (b) Branch sprig: a thin trunk with 8-20 fine twigs, forking dichotomously into a bare-tree crown, about as wide as tall.
- Texture: very smooth, sandblasted, satin; grain follows the limbs in long twisting flutes and ridges; twigs are slender, slightly knobby.
- Colour: red-orange `#b0653a`, mid `#c98a5a`, pale tan `#d9b98d`, dark grain `#6e3b22`; the thin-twig type greyer `#b9a98a`.
- Size: 8-26 in; sprigs 12-24 in tall.

## Cholla wood (`cholla`)
- 1.jpg: aquascaperoom.ca/cholla-wood/ ; img cdn11.bigcommerce.com/s-zdr7z7ycdt/products/523/images/26520/Untitled_30__82773.1766262784.500.750.jpg (3 pieces on white; watermarked)
- 2.jpg: commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cholla_Skeleton_(13051879124).jpg (in situ, desert)
- 3.jpg: commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cholla_Cactus_Skeleton_(25752293774).jpg (in situ, branching)
- Only 1 retail shot; 2-3 are wild photos.
Look:
- Shape: hollow tube or pipe, straight to gently curved, 5-8x longer than wide; open ends; some fork into a Y. The wall is a thin lattice, not solid.
- Texture: wall is a mesh of rounded elongated holes (0.2-0.6 in) in a diamond/spiral pattern, thin ridged webbing between; ragged torn ends; light and fibrous.
- Colour: cream to straw `#d8c08c`, golden tan `#b98f55`, dark inside the tube `#3a2a1c`; bleached ones ivory `#e6ddc4`.
- Size: 4-12 in typical, up to 36 in; 1-2 in diameter.

## Redmoor root wood (`redmoor`)
- 1.jpg: yourlocalfishstore.com/products/red-moor-spider-driftwood ; img cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0837/5104/3390/files/redmoor.png
- 2.jpg: aquasabi.com/Red-Moor-Wood ; img www.aquasabi.de/media/image/product/26851/lg/rote-moorwurzel.jpg

Look:
- Shape: a knotty root crown/base with a dense bush of thin wiry rootlets; 5-15 spindly arms 2-3x the length of the base, curling and branching into many fine tips; some pieces a low wide fan, others a tall crown. Twiggier and finer than spider wood.
- Texture: gnarly rough base with split, peeled patches and root tangles; limbs slender, fibrous, rough.
- Colour: orange-brown `#b9753d`, tan `#d9ac66`, dark brown base `#6a4023`, pale yellow tips `#e6c88c`; polished version redder `#a15a2d`.
- Size: 8-32 in (sold 8-12, 12-20, 20-30 in, up to 60 in).

## Seiryu stone (`seiryu`)
- 1.jpg: yourlocalfishstore.com/products/w-y-s-i-w-y-g-302-black-seiryu-stone-package-2-pieces ; img cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0837/5104/3390/files/library-F5982CE6-C63D-4250-9278-F13FCDDDD21B-L0-001.jpg (dark "black seiryu" pair)
- 2.jpg (saved as 3.jpg): swelluk.com/hugo-kamishi-seiryu-stone ; img www.swelluk.com/media/catalog/product/s/e/seiryu_rock.png (4 shards stacked)
- 2 photos.
Look:
- Shape: angular faceted blocks and tall shards, flat-to-slightly-curved faces and sharp ridges; wedge/shard silhouettes, aspect 1.5-3:1; layered cleaving gives stepped sides. Not rounded.
- Texture: chiselled; jagged ridges, small cracked planes, thin white calcite veins (hairline to 0.2 in) crossing faces; rough-matte with some glossy crystalline spots.
- Colour: blue-grey `#6b7380`, charcoal `#3b3f46`, dark slate `#2a2d33`, light grey `#9aa0a6`; veins `#e8e8e0`.
- Size: 3-14 in per piece; sold by the pound, most 4-10 in.

## Dragon stone / ohko (`dragon`)
- 1.jpg: yourlocalfishstore.com/products/w-y-s-i-w-y-g-305-dragon-stone-3-pieces ; img cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0837/5104/3390/files/library-84E9C715-C0A1-4A4F-8FBB-E87EF7D8B35D-L0-001.jpg (grey/beige, 3 pieces)
- 2.jpg (saved as 3.jpg): aquasabi.com/Ohko-Dragon-Stone ; img www.aquasabi.de/media/image/product/158/lg/drachenstein-ohko.jpg (golden brown, 4 pieces)
- 2 photos.
Look:
- Shape: lumpy irregular blocks and slabs with rounded-off edges, often a broad flat base with a ridged crown; 1-2:1 aspect; some fold into small overhangs.
- Texture: strongly pitted, a honeycomb of shallow scale-like hollows, ridges and holes (0.1-0.6 in), like reptile skin; thin sharp crests with sunken pockets between; eroded, weathered.
- Colour: varied. Olive-grey `#8d8a78`, beige `#bdb39a`, cream highlights `#d8d1b8`; or rust to golden brown `#a8742f`, `#8a5a22`; pockets darker `#4a3a28`.
- Size: 3-12 in per piece; sold by the pound.

## Lava rock (`lava`)
- 1.jpg: commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Vesicular_basalt_(Quaternary;_Snake_River_Plain,_Idaho,_USA)_1.jpg (grey vesicular basalt chunk; geology specimen)
- 2.jpg: swelluk.com/tropica-lava-rock ; img www.swelluk.com/media/catalog/product/s/u/su_tlr815_3_1.png (red-brown lava piece, product shot)
- 2 photos (one is a specimen). Black-lava retail pages (bulkreefsupply, glassaqua) gave only 265 px images or were blocked.
Look:
- Shape: lumpy rounded-angular chunk, no layering and no flat faces; 1-1.5:1 aspect; bumpy outline, lower edge flattish.
- Texture: vesicular, full of round-to-oval gas holes 0.1-1 in (some break the surface), rough crusty skin between, gritty; red type is smoother, cracked, sandy-clay look.
- Colour: black/charcoal `#2b2b2e`, grey `#5a5b60`, pale speckle `#9a9a9e`; red lava brown-red `#8a5a35`, rust `#a0522d`; holes `#141414`.
- Size: 2-10 in per piece (often sold in 15 lb bags).

## Slate (`slate`)
- 1.jpg: aquaticarts.com/products/natural-black-slate-rock-aquarium-terrarium-bonsai-stone ; img cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/1163/2672/products/Black-Slate-3.jpg (stack of slabs)
- 2.jpg: same page ; img cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/1163/2672/products/Black-Slate-1.jpg (single slab)
- 2 photos, no in-tank shot.
Look:
- Shape: thin flat plates (0.25-1 in thick, 4-12 in across), straight cleaved or irregular edges, roughly rectangular/polygonal; stackable into steps; chipped corners with bevelled break edges.
- Texture: faces smooth to finely satin-grained with faint parallel streaks; edges rough with visible fine layers; no pits, no veins.
- Colour: dark grey `#4a4d52`, charcoal `#35373b`, mid grey `#7a7e84` (dusty on the face), slight blue cast; red and green slate also sold.
- Size: 3-12 in wide, 0.3-1 in thick; sold by weight.

## Iron-stained shale / black shale / rust stone (`shale`)
- 1.jpg: commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ferruginous_shale_(GeoDIL_number_-_479).jpg (rust-orange iron-stained slab; specimen)
- 2.jpg: commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Arenaceous_shale_(GeoDIL_number_-_478).jpg (dark green-grey block; unstained look)
- 2 photos, both specimens; no aquarium product shot found (nearest sold names: Black Elderly Stone, Black Ridge Stone).
Look:
- Shape: blocky slabs and flaky plates with a layered, stepped edge; bedding splits into sheets, so pieces are thicker than slate (0.5-3 in), flat faces, angular outline, chipped corners.
- Texture: fine, dusty, gritty; faint bedding lines; crumbly surface with small flakes; rust crust follows the layers and fractures.
- Colour: rust `#b0653a`, orange-brown `#c07040`, tan `#cfa070` over a charcoal/black core `#2e2f33`; unstained olive-grey `#4d544a`.
- Size: 3-12 in, 0.5-3 in thick.

## River stone (`river`)
- 1.jpg: commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Interlocked_pebbles_on_a_river_bed_D35_2285_01.jpg (pebbles underwater)
- 2.jpg: commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wet_multicoloured_beach_pebbles,_Indian_Beach,_Ecola_State_Park,_Oregon.jpg (wet pebbles; shows shape and colour mix)
- 2 photos, both piles, not single pieces (retail pages blocked).
Look:
- Shape: rounded ovals and flattened ellipsoids, 1.2-2:1 aspect, no sharp corners; some egg-shaped, some flat and discoid; gently bulging faces.
- Texture: smooth, tumbled, glossy when wet; fine speckle, occasional quartz stripe; no pits.
- Colour: grey `#6a6e72`, charcoal `#3a3d40`, tan `#a8906f`, brown `#8a6a50`, near-white `#cfcbc0`; darker and glossier when wet.
- Size: 0.5-3 in pebbles; 3-8 in cobbles for hardscape.
