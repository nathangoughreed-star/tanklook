# Terrarium gear prices and species needs (2026-10-26)
Source: search-result snapshots of US retailers (PetSmart, Chewy, Walmart, ReptiFiles, Josh's Frogs, Dubia Roaches, Bio Dude, MorphMarket). Prices undated, verify live. Full data and URLs: terrarium-gear.json.

## Prices (USD)
- Basking bulb 75 W: $10-15, life ~3 mo (Zoo Med rated 2000 h; real range 1.5-6 mo). Incandescent, not halogen.
- Deep dome 8.5 in: $30 (list $40).
- Heat mat (ReptiTherm S/M/L): $25 / $29 / $35.
- On/off thermostat: Inkbird ITC-308 $25-35, Jump Start MTPRTC $49 (up to $94).
- Dimming thermostat: Herpstat 1 about $150 (est, sources conflict).
- UVB T5 HO 24 in kit: $75 (Arcadia ProT5), ReptiSun hood $60-77; tube $25-38, 12 mo.
- Low UVB ShadeDweller 12 in kit: $57-74; tube $20, 12 mo.
- Gauge: $13-22 digital two-probe; IR gun $15-16.
- Hand mister 2 qt: $11-14.
- Auto mister: Monsoon Solo II $135-170, MistKing Starter $180-192.
- Timer: about $8-20 (est).
- Substrate per quart: coco $1.60-2.60, ABG mix $6.25-6.75.

## Labor (all est; no source gives minutes)
Hand mist 1-3 min/session, 1-2 sessions/day for humid species; auto mister refill about 5 min every 2 weeks (keepers refill monthly at most); spot clean about 5 min/week (bioactive about 10).

## Disagreements and gaps
- Heat mats: ReptiFiles-style guidance favors overhead lamps; UK sellers still push mats.
- Dart frog misting: Josh's Azureus sheet says monthly hand misting with a mister system; many say daily. UVB disputed (dartfrog.pet against).
- Anole UVB bulb life: 6 vs 12 months. No ReptiFiles page found for brown anole, White's tree frog or inverts; Tom's Big Spiders not reached.
