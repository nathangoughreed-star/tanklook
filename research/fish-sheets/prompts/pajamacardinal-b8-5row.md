Create a scientifically accurate orthographic reference sheet of a single adult pajama cardinalfish (Sphaeramia nematoptera) for 3D model reconstruction.

PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

This is a technical reconstruction reference, not a collection of separate fish illustrations.

Every view must represent the exact same rigid three-dimensional specimen, with identical body proportions, fin attachment positions, anatomical landmarks, and coloration.

Priority order:

1. Accurate species anatomy and body proportions.
2. Exact silhouette consistency between matching views.
3. Correct fin geometry and attachment boundaries.
4. Consistent anatomical landmarks and coloration.
5. Surface detail and illustration quality.

If any requirements conflict, prioritize geometry over visual appearance.
CANVAS AND LAYOUT

* Portrait canvas, 2:3 width-to-height ratio.
* Pure black (#000000) background.
* Five horizontal rows separated by at least 3% of the canvas height of empty black space.
* Row 4 contains two front views side by side, separated by black space.
* All views use exactly the same physical scale.
* No perspective distortion, labels, rulers, borders, shadows, or additional objects.
* Keep all fins and body parts within their assigned view areas.
* Center the views consistently. Side and top views must share the same horizontal snout and caudal-peduncle coordinates.

MASTER GEOMETRY — MOST IMPORTANT
First establish one anatomically accurate, finless three-dimensional body. All five rows must be derived from this same body.

Species proportions:

* Adult pajama cardinalfish, standard length 2.7 inches.
* Maximum body depth = approximately 54% of standard length.
* Head depth = approximately 33% of standard length.
* Minimum caudal-peduncle depth = approximately 16% of standard length.
* Adult. Cardinalfish body, deep and laterally compressed, with an arched back and a convex belly.
* The head is large; the profile rises evenly to the first dorsal fin.
* Greatest body depth at the start of the first dorsal fin.
* Caudal peduncle short and slender.
* Eye round, very large, in the upper half of the head.
* Mouth large, terminal, closed.
* Snout blunt and gently rounded in the side and top views, never a pointed cone or beak.
* Seen from the front, the closed mouth is a fine lip line on a solid head surface; the nostrils are faint marks on the skin, never dark holes or openings.
* Natural asymmetry of the head and body profile; do not substitute a perfect ellipse or pointed leaf shape.

Important: The front and top views must reflect this laterally compressed anatomy. Do not draw a round disc and do not inflate the body into a cylinder.
MASTER COLORATION
Natural colouring, slightly subdued, not saturated.

The head and front of the body are greenish-yellow. A broad, dark vertical band crosses the body behind the head, below the first dorsal fin, and the scales inside this band have black edges. The rear body is silver-white with scattered orange-brown polka dots.

Eye: bright red-orange with a dark pupil.

Color boundaries must wrap continuously over the three-dimensional body, remaining consistent in all projections.
FIN GEOMETRY
Attach the fins to the established master body without modifying its shape.

* First dorsal: A tall, triangular fin of spines, with the first ray drawn out into a long filament. Pale yellow with a dark front edge. Drawn opaque.
* Second dorsal: A separate soft fin behind the first, rounded at the rear. Pale yellow, with a dark edge. Drawn opaque.
* Anal: Starts under the second dorsal, rounded at the rear. Pale yellow. Drawn opaque.
* Caudal: Slightly forked, with rounded lobes. Pale yellow-silver. Drawn opaque.
* Pectoral: Emerging behind the gill cover, rounded, naturally folded close against the flank. Clear. Drawn opaque.
* Pelvic: Pointed, below the pectoral base. Pale yellow. Drawn opaque.
* No adipose fin.

Use natural fin-ray structure and smooth, anatomically accurate edges. Fins are opaque and not translucent.

Record each fin's precise attachment boundary to the master body. These attachment boundaries must remain unchanged in every panel.
