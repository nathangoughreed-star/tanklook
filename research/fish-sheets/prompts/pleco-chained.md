# Pleco: four chained prompts (never run)

Not run: Nathan judged it too costly for 150+ fish. Recorded for completeness.

## 1 side


```text
Create ONE image on a pure black background: a true side elevation (orthographic, camera at infinity, no perspective) of the BODY ONLY of an adult common pleco (Hypostomus plecostomus), left flank, nose pointing right, body axis perfectly horizontal. Remove every fin: no pectoral, pelvic, dorsal, anal, adipose or tail fin; leave the tail as a short bare stub. Photorealistic and anatomically faithful, flat even light, no shadows, no glow, no text, no labels, no panel titles, nothing but the fish. Mouth underneath, hidden. Plated flank with dark brown spots on a lighter olive-brown ground. Proportions, as fractions of body length snout to tail stub: body depth about 0.17, head depth about 0.14. Make the fish as wide as the canvas allows (about 1000 px long).
```

## 2 top

```text
Now render the SAME fish, the exact same individual, seen straight down from above (orthographic). Identical length: snout and tail stub must line up horizontally with the side view, same scale, same pattern. Same fins removed. Proportions: head width about 0.22 of body length, maximum body width about 0.25. The width of the head you draw here is the width the head must have in the front view too. No text, no labels. Place it on a separate image, black background.
```

## 3 front

```text
Now render the SAME fish seen straight from the front (orthographic, camera at infinity, not close-up), at exactly the same scale as the previous two images, so one pixel is the same length in all three. Its width must equal the head width in the top view (about 0.22 of body length at the head, 0.25 at the widest) and its height must equal the body depth in the side view (about 0.17). The eyes must be at the same height as in the side view. The sucker mouth is visible underneath. No text, no labels.
```

## 4 fins

```text
Now make a reference sheet of the fins of this same pleco on a pure black background, at the SAME scale as the body images (the tail fin's base must be exactly as tall as the tail stub of the body). One fin per row, each laid flat and fully spread, base edge toward the left, tip toward the right, nothing overlapping, wide black gaps. Fins in this order: dorsal, pectoral, pelvic, anal, caudal, adipose. Same colour and spots as the body. Flat even light, no shadows, no text, no labels, no body.
```
