Stocking survey v4

Stocking and compatibility survey, v4

60 tanks in four modules, each described in words (no picture: a camera angle or the decor changes how full a tank looks). Answer only the modules you know; skip any tank whose animals you do not know. Every question is a button except the two count boxes. Answers save in this browser as you go; Export answers downloads a file to send back.

The scale. Ideal = the stocking an experienced keeper aims for (reads 75 % in the app). At most = the count past which long-term problems become likely (reads 100 %). Caution lies between the two.

Assume: adults at the lengths shown, no breeding counted, sexes as listed. Filtration, water changes, heating, lighting and furnishing (plants, cover, hides, sight breaks, rockwork) are whatever these animals need: never the limit. Judge these animals, at these counts, in this tank.
