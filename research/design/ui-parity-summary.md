# PA1 summary: what the new shell left behind (2026-11-17)

Full audit: `research/design/ui-parity-audit.md` (169 control groups, ranked gaps, method). Pictures: `research/screens/PA1/`.

**Counts (control groups):** PRESENT 87, MOVED 32, CHANGED 16, MISSING 31, CUT on purpose 3. About three quarters of the old UI is in the shell; the missing 31 cluster in four places: the **drawers** (Habitat and Cost lost their detail blocks), **Constraints / Preferences** (brief fields with no control), the **Tank screen** (background pictures, arc, outlines, table position, stand finish) and **Light map options**.

**Top 10 gaps, most to least valuable**
1. Habitat check: "What counts" load table and the ammonia / nitrogen budget (feeding, tap nitrate, plants' share, filter capacity, nitrate water change), crowd and cover advice. M.
2. Cost drawer: "Same look, three builds" (Budget / As designed / Luxury), kit "Show as Tank B", owned-heater deal, animals' share line. M.
3. Open-top lid warning with the one-click "Add a glass top" and lid-free pros and cons. S-M.
4. Heater / chiller "can it hold the temperature" and "room warmer than the tank" warnings. M.
5. Constraints fields the brief still has: set-up hours, tank size range, hardness and pH, "What I want from it", the "if it does not all fit" setting, ranges and Target vs Firm, sales tax / phasing. M.
6. Light map: aquascape shading, dark spots, expected plant outcomes. S.
7. Preferences: size slider, Avoid on looks, CO2-system and no-plants exclusions. S-M.
8. Water condition (algae look) slider. S.
9. Tank screen (BG1's list): background pictures, round-tank arc, glass outline lines. S-M.
10. Furniture table position and Centre / Back / Front, stand finish; Delete all animals; AR button. S each.

**Not a gap (cut on purpose, UI2 §2b):** Plan sheet and Build plan, Coach, Quick Setup block, second Total Power slider, Open chooser, Guardrails dialog.

**Confidence:** about 60 % of rows were seen live on both sides ("V" in the table); the rest were read in source ("i"). Compare, Start / Save dialogs, terrarium controls and the bow / pie / bowl maker states were not driven.
