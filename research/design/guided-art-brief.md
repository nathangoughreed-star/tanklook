# Guided art brief (Nathan makes these separately; 2026-10-30)

AI-generated art, NOT engine renders. Realistic and crisp, not cartoony (the painted-fish style was rejected twice).
Drop each file into `public/art/guided/` with the exact name below; the Guided cards (lane GV1) pick it up, otherwise they show a placeholder.
Format: .webp, 1200 x 750 px (16:10), under ~150 KB each. The card crops to 16:10 and also shows a 1:1 crop on phones, so keep the subject centred.

## Step 1: What kind of tank?
| File | Picture | Card line |
|---|---|---|
| `kind-freshwater.webp` | A planted freshwater aquarium with a school of small fish | Fish and plants in fresh water: the easiest place to start. |
| `kind-saltwater.webp` | A reef tank with corals, clownfish, live rock | Marine fish, corals and invertebrates: more gear and care. |
| `kind-terrarium.webp` | A glass terrarium with a gecko or dart frog, moss, branches | Land animals and plants in a closed enclosure, no open water. |
| `kind-paludarium.webp` | Half land, half water: a waterfall, plants above, fish below | Land and water in one tank: the most involved. |
| `kind-any.webp` | A loose collage or a row of all four, softly blended | Not sure yet: show me options of every kind. |

## Step 2: Where do you want to start?
| File | Picture | Card line |
|---|---|---|
| `intent-animals.webp` | An ecosystem of fish: several species at different levels of one tank, the animals are the focus | Start from the animals you want to keep. |
| `intent-look.webp` | An overall front view of a beautiful finished aquascape | Start from how you want it to look. |
| `intent-equipment.webp` | A loose assorted pile of miscellaneous aquarium gear: a used tank, light, heater, filter, hoses | Start from gear you already have. |
| `intent-ideas.webp` | Several small different tanks side by side, or a sketchbook of ideas | Show me a few ideas and I'll pick. |
