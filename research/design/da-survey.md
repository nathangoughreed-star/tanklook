# Survey for the Discovery-Algorithm (DA) architecture (read-only inspection of origin/main d7b7d433, 2026-11-09)

Answers section 10 of `tanklook-discovery-architecture-handoff.md`. Line numbers are as read; verify before editing. Short version: the repo already holds a persistent `Brief` with a `locked` status, non-destructive "update, don't replace" helpers (`handOff`, `adaptToTank`, `applyChange`), an independent explore gate, and an owned-gear model. What is missing is (a) per-object locks on the scene, (b) scope (tab) enforcement, (c) a delta/proposal type, (d) a revision check, and (e) a single verdict entry point.

## 1. Project / scene state

- Types: `Scene` (`src/scene/types.ts:458-468`: `version, name, units, tanks[], active, camLock`) holds one tank, or two after a split. `TankSetup` (`types.ts:386-432`) is the design: `tank, camera, render, light, substrate, layout, terrain, water, lid, stand, wall, person, backdrop, grow, condition, fish[], items[], patches?, tech?, feeding?, pairs?, integrated?, product?, constraints?, brief?`. `SCENE_VERSION = 9` (`types.ts:7`).
- There is no separate "Project" object. The project = the active `TankSetup` plus its `brief?: Brief`. The brief (`src/scene/brief.ts`, `BRIEF_VERSION = 5` at :126; `Brief {v, items, dialog?, vision?, project?}`) carries what the user wants and has. Its `Values` (`brief.ts:52-84`) are the keys `animals.stock`, `appearance.look`, `equipment.tank`, `constraints.*` (budget, monthly, careMin, setupHours, maxSpace, tankGal, existingTank, owned), `preferences.*`. `ProjectExtras` (`brief.ts:148`) adds world, water, route, scratch, skipped, `use` (Required/Available per equip key), tankDims, coach progress.
- `src/scene/project.ts` is NOT a state type; it is the pure selector/patch layer over the Brief: `projectOf`, `enclosureOf` (:279), `conflictsOf` (:317), `unresolved`, `assumptionsInUse`, `applyChange(b, patch: ProjectPatch)` (:547) which returns `{state, conflicts, introduced, resolved}` and keeps every downstream pick.
- Stable IDs: `Fish.id: number` (`types.ts:72`), `Item.id: number` (:283), `Patch.id` (:~420), all unique per tank only. `Store.nextId()` (`store.ts:147`) gives the next fish id. `regenerate()` and `shuffleHard()` renumber items (`starter.ts:183`, :232-247), so ids are NOT stable across generation. No ids exist for substrate zones: the substrate is a single `SubstrateSettings` (`types.ts:202`: type, `custom` blend, four corner depths `fl/fr/bl/br`, `litter`) plus a `terrain` height grid and painted `patches`. Hardscape and plants are both `Item` (`kind: stone|wood|cave|plant|coral|artificial`). Animals are individual `Fish` rows, not groups. Groups are derived (`scene/duplicate.ts`); the brief's `Stocked {species,count,f,u,pair,tl}` is the species-level roster.
- Persistence: `Store.saveLocal/loadLocal` write one JSON of the whole `Scene` to localStorage key `tanklook.scene` (`store.ts:29,328-336`; `main.ts:34`). Named saves live in `src/scene/library.ts` (localStorage `tanklook.lib.*`), file open/save and share links go through `parseScene` (`src/scene/validate.ts:115`) with `migrate` (:76; throws on newer versions). The brief is parsed by `parseBrief` (`validate.ts:372`). There is no project id, no revision counter, and no history beyond undo (below). User profile/limits are separate: `loadProfile` (`ui/demandPanel.ts:36`, localStorage) and `scene/constraints.ts` (`lastUsed`/`rememberLastUsed`, per-tank `TankSetup.constraints`). Explore favourites/candidates are in `tanklook.explore` (`explore/store.ts:13`).
- Undo/redo: `Store` (`src/scene/store.ts`). `update(fn, {kind, history, coalesce})` (:184) pushes `JSON.stringify(scene)` snapshots onto `undoStack` (`pushUndo` :258, MAX_HISTORY 200), coalescing by key within 1200 ms. `replace(scene)` (:318) is one undo step and resets `loadDepth` so `previousTank` ignores history older than the replace. `mark`/`squash`/`rollback` (:276-283) give preview-then-keep. Undo restores the whole scene (the camera is kept). Whole-scene snapshots, so a per-run proposal undo would be one `update` call. Store also runs invariants after every scene edit (:~195: `holdIntegrated, prunePairs, keepInWater, fitTable ...`), a natural place to hang a lock-enforcement check.

## 3. Existing lock-like concepts

- Brief item `status: 'hard' | 'preference' | 'locked'` (`brief.ts:21`; `lock/unlock/lockedIds` :263-266; UI `setupBrief.ts:190 toggleLock`, `lockRoster` :199, `lockAnimals` :192, `briefBar.ts:75`). Per item (whole value), not per field. Only the locked ROSTER (`lockedRoster` `setupBrief.ts:94`) has any enforcement: `handOff` reconciles the scene's fish to it (`reeval.ts:209-229`, `lockedRosterFor` :241, `reconcileRoster` :142). `Stocked.count` is exact; there is no "at least n".
- `Brief.items[id].flex` = a limit the user will bend (`setFlex`, `isFlex` :230-234). Hard vs flexible constraints (`ConstraintKind`, `DEFAULT_LIMIT_KINDS` `project.ts:35`). Experience is advisory.
- Equipment `Use = 'required' | 'available'` per key (`brief.ts:138`, `project.ts:128-130 requiredEquipment/availableEquipment`, `equipUseOf`); `Required` is honoured by `ownedAssess` (`demand/suggest.ts:114`, `REQUIRED_KEPT` :375) and the tank (`enclosureOf` priority owned-required > chosen > owned-available). EQ1 (2026-11-07) made owned gear single-state "available" in the UI (`ownedFit.ts`, `DEFAULT_OWNED_USE` `project.ts:39`) but the `required` plumbing is still there.
- Scene flags: `light.owned`, `light.paidUsd`, tech units `owned` (`scene/tech.ts`), `stockFirst`, `integrated` (held by `holdIntegrated`, released on size change), `product`, `pairs` (pruned by `prunePairs` when the two fish change), `layout.gatePlants/gateAnimals/gateStock`, `layout.careSet`. `layout.seed/plantSeed/animalSeed/gSeed` pin a layer's random draw, so redrawing one layer leaves the others (Shuffle with parts locked, `starter.ts:232`). These are not user locks.
- Kept-through-regenerate: only open-top garden pieces (`starter.ts:185`) and, in `handOff`, tank/gear/roster (`reeval.ts:174-236`). `applyChange` keeps every downstream pick and lists conflicts with `fixes` (`project.ts:547`). `explore/store.ts:25 isProtected` protects liked/saved candidates from eviction.
- Nothing locks a hardscape piece, plant group, substrate area, or tank dimension, and nothing is scoped by tab.

## 4. Validation independent of generation

There is NO single entry point. The current project's verdicts come from several pure functions called by different UI pieces:

- Stocking and bioload: `scene/stocking.ts:301 stockingOf(S)` (memo `:368 stockingMemo`), space/filtration percent, `isOver` (:392).
- Compat/welfare summary (five categories minimums/social/environment/behaviour/fullness): `ui/stockSummary.ts:210 summaryOf(S, units)` which calls `stockSummary` (:85), `tankMinimum` (`scene/tankMin.ts`), `minWarnings` (:183). Underlying: `scene/animalfit.ts:42 whyAnimal/whysAnimal` (:48), `groupMin.ts`, `herpcheck.ts:50 herpCheck`, `growcheck.ts:22 growthCheck`, `lightcheck.ts` (plant PAR verdicts), `scene/temprange.ts`, `scene/nitrogen.ts`.
- Equipment sufficiency: `scene/ownedFit.ts:19 ownedFit(S, brief)` returns notes (owned tank unused/small, light dim/unused, heater small, filter small); surfaced by `ui/equipmentWarn.ts`. Needed-gear logic: `demand/needs.ts:108 needsOf`, `scene/setupNeeds.ts applySetupNeeds` (adds light/CO2/ferts; runs inside `handOff`), `scene/tech.ts` (`filterUnits, heaterUnits, heatLoss`).
- Limits/brief: `scene/constraints.ts:103 compliance(S, totals, profile, units)` gives startup/monthly/time/space pass/over/unknown. `demand/cost.ts:14 limitState`. Brief-level: `project.ts:317 conflictsOf`, `:379 feasibilityOf` (kept-but-infeasible items), `:420 unresolved`; UI `ui/briefFeasibility.ts`. `scene/tankfit.ts:286 fitIn` / `tankOptions` check a roster against a tank.
- The nearest thing to "one verdict": `explore/gate.ts:30 gateCandidate(GateInput)` -> `{verdict: eligible|provisional|blocked, gates[welfare, equipment, world, budget, monthly, care, setup, space, size, lower], reasons}`. It already separates welfare (`kind:'rule'`, never traded for money) from flexible/firm limits. But it takes a `ConceptEval` + `Estimate` + `Wants` built by `demand/suggest.ts:635 evaluateCandidate`, not a TankSetup. A thin `verdictOf(project)` composing the functions above (and reusing `gateCandidate` for the gate shape) is the missing piece. Hard-vs-welfare separation already exists in the gate and in `GuardResult` (`constraints.ts:89`).

## 8. Gaps against invariants 1-12

1. Unchecked means unchanged: missing. No scope concept; `regenerate(s,'all')` (`starter.ts:183`) rewrites several tabs. File: new `src/scene/scope.ts`, plus guarding `starter.ts`, `reeval.ts`.
2. Locks survive checked scope (exact vs at least): partial. Roster is lockable and enforced in `handOff` (exact only; `Stocked.count`). Need min/exact semantics: `brief.ts` (`Stocked`), `reeval.ts reconcileRoster`.
3. Fine-grained placement locks: missing. No flags on `Item`/`Fish`/substrate/terrain; ids unstable (`starter.ts`). Files: `types.ts` (a `locks` structure on `TankSetup`), `starter.ts`, `validate.ts`.
4. No silent changes (preview/reject never mutates; apply does not lock): partial. Explore previews do not write; Suggest thumbnails and Visual Discovery `shot()` mutate the live store temporarily (`suggest.ts:196`, `panels.ts:315`). Apply does not auto-lock but `makeItMine`/`lockRoster` paths (`discovery/screens.ts`, `infeasibleCard.ts:130`) lock animals on adoption. Files: those plus a pure render-of-candidate path (`render/tankView.ts` ghost exists).
5. Equipment distinction: partial. Required/available + owned exist in the brief; UI single-state (EQ1); `buyGroups` derives purchases; per-candidate purchase and acceptance flow absent. Files: `project.ts`, `ownedGear.ts`, `demand/suggest.ts ownedAssess`.
6. Constraint conflicts explicit: partial/mostly supported. `conflictsOf`, `feasibilityOf`, `ownedFit`, `setupNeeds` (flags weak owned light), `explainInfeasible`. Gap: the plant demand vs required weak light is flagged but not blocking in explore (`gate.ts` equipment gate; check that it covers PAR).
7. Cross-tab context: partial. `handOff` + `applySetupNeeds` read the whole tank; pool/gate reads brief and roster. A scope-limited run must read everything and write only in scope.
8. Actual remaining cost: supported. Owned gear zeroes price in `engine.ts`; `toBuy`/`buyGroups`. Gap: "available but inadequate" must not count; `ownedFit` notes it but `demandOf` credits owned gear regardless of capability (`engine.ts:129`).
9. Scoped refinement: missing. `swapsFor/applySwap` is narrow, but no region/objective filter; `regenerate` is layer-wide.
10. No-solution state: supported for Explore/Suggest (`explore/pool.ts:133 noResultOf`, `infeasible.ts`, `ui/explore/notes.ts`); not lock-aware (no locks), and relaxations apply only to limits (`AltEdit`).
11. Persistence/re-entry on one project: partial. One saved Scene with brief; but W1-W10 replace it, and the Guided/Sandbox mode flag is a global localStorage preference (`startMode.ts:15`) not a project field. Files: `panels.ts`, `buildPick.ts`, `suggest.ts`.
12. Proposal integrity (no stale apply): missing. No revision counter on `Scene`/`Store`; `explore/candidate.ts` snapshots are stale-checked only against limits (`explore/store.ts:30 recheckCandidate`). Files: `store.ts` (add `rev`), new `src/scene/proposal.ts`.

## 9. Suggested lane split

Disjoint file ownership; DA0 first, then three parallel lanes, then one integration lane. A concern: `src/ui/panels.ts` is touched by every UI lane, so edit it only through small hook functions (mount calls), one owner per phase.

- DA0 (sequential, small): foundation types and pure core, no UI. New `src/scene/locks.ts` (lock record keyed by stable id/field; exact vs min; derived "is protected" queries), `src/scene/scope.ts` (tab set `tank|animals|plants|hardscape|substrate`, `editable = scope ∩ unlocked`, `fieldsTouched(before, after)`, `assertWithinScope`), `src/scene/proposal.ts` (`Proposal = delta over TankSetup + brief` with `baseRev`, preview/apply, `checkStale`), and `Store.rev` (`store.ts`) + `locks?` on `TankSetup` (`types.ts`, `validate.ts` parse/migrate, SCENE_VERSION bump or no-bump optional field per house style). Also stable ids for `Item` across `regenerate` (`starter.ts`, `shuffleHard`): keep an item's id when a layer redraw keeps it. Tests: immutability invariants 1-3, 12 (`test/*.test.ts`, vitest).
- DA1 (parallel after DA0): unified validation. New `src/scene/verdict.ts` `verdictOf(project)` composing `stockingOf`, `summaryOf`/`whysAnimal`, `herpCheck`, `growthCheck`, `lightFitNote`, `ownedFit`, `needsOf`, `compliance`, `conflictsOf` into one `{welfare, compat, equipment, limits, conflicts}` result, reusing `gateCandidate`'s hard/flex/welfare split. Owns new file only, plus a read-only "Checks" strip in a new `ui/checks.ts`. Files read: scene/*, `ui/stockSummary.ts`, `explore/gate.ts` (no edits).
- DA2 (parallel after DA0): scoped generators. Add scope+lock-aware wrappers around `regenerate`/`handOff`/`applySwap`/`reconcileRoster` returning `Proposal` deltas; make `suggestSetups`/`explorePool` candidates convertible to deltas (`snapshot` vs current TankSetup diff, trimmed to scope, rejected if a lock is touched). Owns `scene/starter.ts`, `scene/reeval.ts`, `demand/swaps.ts`, `explore/pool.ts`/`candidate.ts` (additive), plus new `src/explore/scoped.ts`. Depends on DA0 types; independent of DA1/DA3.
- DA3 (parallel after DA0): equipment model and plan. Surface `Use=required` ("Must use"), have-available, to-purchase per candidate: `scene/project.ts` equipment selectors, `ui/ownedGear.ts`, `ui/myEquipment.ts`, `demand/suggest.ts ownedAssess` (do not credit inadequate gear: engine fix at `demand/engine.ts:129`), `demand/buildplan.ts`. Owns those; no overlap with DA2 except through the `Proposal` type.
