# 3D backgrounds: reference photos and written looks (BD1, R199, 2026-11-27)

Nathan 2026-11-27: "now that the graphics have greatly improved in the fish and plants, the 3d backgrounds are looking out of
place with their low quality". Method per `research/species-art-method.md`: real photos first, look written down, render beside
the photo. Target: the scene's stylization level (R144), not cartoony, not photographic.

## What the old relief got wrong (all styles)
- Colour and shading lived on the vertices only (3-8 mm grid, about 5 px per vertex at the default front view), so every edge
  smeared. Real backgrounds read through **crisp edges and dark joints**, sharper than anything on the grid.
- Pieces were rounded blobs (superellipses, domes) of one size. Real walls mix big pieces with small, and the rock ones are
  **flat faces broken at sharp edges**.
- Low contrast: hollows only 50 % darker. In every photo the gaps between pieces go nearly black.
- Light baked from a fixed direction, so the wall ignores the scene light (VF2-A).

facets; blocks stacked in rough horizontal courses, top faces often tilted up (catch the light), undersides in shadow; joints
between blocks deep and near-black; hairline cracks across faces; colour one family (grey, or brown/rust) with gentle
mottling and pale dust on upward faces.
