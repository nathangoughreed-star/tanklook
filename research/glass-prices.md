Float = ordinary clear plate. Low-iron = Starphire / Optiwhite class. Rule of thumb used: low-iron = float x 1.5 to 1.7
(Hammerhead: 20-100% premium; fastaquatics: +40-60%; Lemieux: +14% to +60%).

## Surcharges
- Polished edge: $3-6 per linear ft (fastaquatics, 2026-11); $3 / $6 / $12 low / avg / high (latestcost). Lemieux includes it in its price.
  Default: $4 per linear ft for polished edge; plain seamed (arrised) edge is normally free to a few cents; aquarium builds usually use seamed edges.
- Tempering: fastaquatics says $50-100 setup plus 20-30% on the $/sq ft. Lemieux says "free" (bundled). latestcost prices tempered at
  $25-80/sq ft (avg 45) vs annealed $10-45 (avg 25), about +80%. Default: +25% plus $75 setup per order. Note aquarium panes are normally annealed, not tempered (tempered cannot be cut or drilled afterward).
- Big box (Home Depot / Lowe's): sell only pre-cut stock, no cut-to-size service. Home Depot clear sheets (e.g. 24x36) are listed at 0.093 in (3/32) or 0.125 in (1/8);
  Lowe's Gardner 24x36 is 3/32. Prices not retrievable (both sites return HTTP 403 to automated fetch). Only price seen: Fab Glass 12x18 in 1/8 in TEMPERED polished-edge table-top piece, about $90.71 (about $60/sq ft, not comparable to annealed stock).
  Hardware-store single/double-strength sheets typically run roughly $3-6/sq ft (my estimate, unverified).

## Caveats
- Sources disagree by 2-3x. Most are content-farm estimates, not shop price lists; only Lemieux is a real shop and it bundles tempering and polish.
- Large-format 3/4 in to 1 in aquarium glass is often quoted per piece with freight and crating; per-sq-ft defaults understate small orders and overstate large ones.
- Low-iron prices swing with supply and with shop mark-up; some shops only quote low-iron at 3/8 in and thicker.
- 3/32, 3/16, 5/8 and 1 in rows are interpolated or estimated; no source priced them. 1/8 in low-iron is also an estimate.
- Not included: freight, crating, cutouts/holes, tax, minimum order charges (often $50-100).
- Every price in the app should be shown as sourced default estimate plus a user override field (house rule).
