For lights, assume a cheap/basic light until plants are added that would require more." Plants that need more also bring their light cost and ferts / CO2 into the price.
Today: a stock-first tank sets filter and heater to 'none' (src/scene/tech.ts ~180, `inferTech`) and stays bare; Suggest sizes gear itself. The default light is a fixed flat fixture (src/scene/defaults.ts ~24) that never upgrades; demanding plants only produce a warning (src/demand/scapes.ts ~211).
Build:
1. `src/demand/needs.ts` (new), contract fixed in the plan: `Need { kind: 'tank' | 'heater' | 'filter' | 'light' | 'co2' | 'ferts'; why; species; have; fix?: { label; deltaUsd } }`, `needsOf(S, brief?)`. Heater when any species' minimum temp is above the room temp (brief `roomTempC`, shown in deg F; default room 70 deg F when unset, and say so in `why`). 2. Stock-first tanks get exactly the needed gear, sized (heater by heat loss, filter by gallons, light by tier), and nothing before a need exists. Owned gear (constraints.owned / a unit's `owned`) satisfies a need at $0.
